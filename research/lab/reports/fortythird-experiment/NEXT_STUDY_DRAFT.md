## Proposed CPU comparison after intake

Use a cleanly initialized conventional 2D learner and a capacity-, context-, label- and update-matched copy. One receives native height; the other receives the same height after fixed 4× lateral block averaging and upsampling to the same grid. Train each at its observation quality and test both input qualities on the same reserved papyrus, with no checkpoint/threshold selection on that parent. Compare their exact-grid AP and a prespecified overlap metric on common valid support. Evaluate simple mean/missingness-only controls so interpolation or missing-value patterns cannot masquerade as ink learning. Retain training-fit checks to distinguish an inadequate learner from failed transfer. A full pixel/degradation/normalization and budget contract still needs the real-file intake; this draft is not a training GO.

The native arm is the positive control. If it cannot fit supported training labels at the frozen adequate CPU budget, no acquisition-quality conclusion follows. If it fits but does not transfer across parents, prioritize coverage/representation. If it transfers and a matched-quality 4× learner loses useful performance, a native-teacher distillation arm becomes a justified next hypothesis. If matched-quality training closes the gap, prioritize acquisition-matched learning. Do not run a teacher arm first or replace negative results with architecture sweeps.

## What calibration uncertainty allows

The paper reports 0.34µm native lateral sampling. The release reports 0.68793625µm, and its September 3 notice explicitly says the inconsistency is under investigation. Manifest extents approximately match the latter at pixel centers (maximum arithmetic residual 0.000175µm), but that arithmetic does not settle instrument calibration. Express this supporting experiment in **relative factors 1× and 4×**, not an asserted physical resolution cutoff. Avoid physical-height quantization tied to the disputed lateral units. Degradation ratios and label agreement remain testable after image QC; claims that ink becomes unrecoverable above a particular number of micrometres do not.

A successful topography control would justify investigating matched-observation learning and distillation. It would not demonstrate CT contrast, CT registration, closed-scroll transfer or frontier novelty.
