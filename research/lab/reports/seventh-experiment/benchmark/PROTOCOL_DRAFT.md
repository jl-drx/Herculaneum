The question is whether the sixth study's ex016 ranking/false-positive disadvantage recurs on other-scroll annotation-supported contexts, and whether a development-locked operating point transfers. It does not test the general value of pseudo-labeling or establish authentic ink. The four exact retained arms remain ex016 and its matched manual control, plus official seed42 and seed43. No checkpoint shopping, ensemble, depth fitting or reserved-region threshold tuning is included.
