# Bounded benchmark expansion: source contract first

Frozen before new network requests, images, label arrays or model predictions. Root owns scientific design. The objective is to construct a more informative benchmark with additional physical fragments and calibration/evaluation roles separated before prediction. Current Plinius models, Frag4 diagnostics, all prior evidence and tracked repository files remain unchanged.

## Candidate universe and role policy

Audit the official EduceLab legacy Frag1, Frag2, Frag3, Frag5 and Frag6 releases. Frag4 is already seen diagnostic evidence and will not become calibration or new validation data. Reuse its retained publication/source contract without new payloads. Reuse the official full catalog and EduceLab-Scrolls paper already retained; do not fetch a whole catalog again.

The paper identifies Frag1=PHercParis2Fr47 and Frag2=PHercParis2Fr143 as pieces of the same parent papyrus, and Frag3=PHercParis1Fr34 and Frag4=PHercParis1Fr39 as pieces of another. Physical fragments, parent papyri, scan energies and cropped contexts are different grouping levels. Repeated scans or crops must not inflate the independent specimen count.

Provisional role preference, based on identity and existing exposure rather than new labels or scores: use eligible Frag3/Paris1 support for domain calibration, reserve eligible Frag1/Frag2/Paris2 support for evaluation, and retain Frag4/Paris1 as seen diagnostics. This keeps the known parent-papyrus groups separate between new calibration and evaluation. Audit Frag5 and Frag6 as additional source candidates, with explicit prior parent exposure. Frag5 shares PHerc1667, already used in model development. Assign any additional final role only from metadata/exposure evidence before label/image values or predictions; otherwise defer it. Historical upstream exposure remains qualified, not assumed absent.

## Stage 1: metadata and source evidence only

Use retained local metadata first. New requests are public verified-TLS HTTPS reads of official release directories, small JSON descriptors/render/volume metadata, text source and license documents. Preferred host dl.ash2txt.org; official alternatives require explicit recorded source links and the same budget. No credentials, account operations, outreach, terms acceptance, uploads or paid resources.

Budget: at most 80 network attempts, 4 MiB response bodies in total, 256 KiB per response. No automatic retries or redirects. Record every attempt, status, body size, ETag/Last-Modified where supplied, SHA-256 and completion state. Do not substitute partial/truncated metadata for a complete source. No CT/label/optical payloads or image-header ranges in this stage. Existing public license text may be read and compared; retrieving it is not an acceptance or authorization to redistribute data.

For each candidate establish aliases and parent grouping; exact release volume/surface/render identity; scan energy and voxel spacing; declared stack dimensions/dtype; label/surface-mask/registered-IR associations; annotation and zero/nonzero participation convention; publication-backed depth/normal convention and limits; range-reader feasibility from known format/descriptor metadata; data-use/citation restrictions; and our local plus upstream exposure status. Directory availability alone does not prove label alignment or annotation depth. File-name energy or plane ranges must be corroborated where possible. Do not apply modern mapping-validity semantics to legacy surface masks.

Source-backed unknown registration residual or upstream ancestry may permit a qualified supplied-reference comparison. Missing source/label association or an indefensible depth window cannot be invented. PHerc0009B's unresolved annotation-depth/transfer/mapping-validity contract remains deferred; do not restart its already documented source search here.

## Stage 2 boundary

After this audit, root will issue explicit eligibility decisions and a separate frozen benchmark/label-acquisition contract, including exact source pins, final roles, image budget, deterministic crop-selection rule, boundary exclusions, class-support requirements and acquisition stop conditions. That contract must precede any new label/image decoding. Where eligible sources suffice, bounded label-only validation can then make the benchmark concrete; all CT inference, calibration score fitting and training remain deferred until their own frozen execution plan. No threshold, crop, depth, orientation or model may be selected using new predictions.

No automatic switch to another candidate after a failed label/support gate. Record a failure and its implication, preserving the predeclared role assignment. No claim of a pristine upstream holdout follows from project-level reservation. Keep local data private and attach no text-reading claims to metadata or labels.

When stage2 is feasible, retain deterministic selected support and its quality receipts without model predictions. Independently check identities, bounds, split exclusions, counts and source pins. Freeze previous evidence and append a research register/local integrity manifest after completing the phase.

No GPU resource or worker secret is needed. New model development should follow this benchmark contract, rather than another loss/rate/cutoff sweep over the same diagnostics.
