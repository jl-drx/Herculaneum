## Local adapter acceptance

Provide a small NumPy column-block scoring API, not a production volume loader. Inputs must declare probability encoding, exact Boolean validity, selection, and known/unknown labels. Unknown labels are encoded as2; they must never enter the negative class. Zero with valid=true remains a legitimate prediction. Invalid payload values may be arbitrary but must remain excluded. Nonfinite/out-of-range values on declared valid support, shape mismatches, unsupported encodings, fractional layer coordinates, non-Boolean masks and oversized arrays must raise. Support float0–1, uint8/255, both-endian uint16/65535 and explicit label012 encodings. Do not infer uint8 0/1 as a binary mask. Limit scalar inputs to1,000,000 elements and depth blocks to4,000,000 voxels.

Compute exact tie-aware AUC/AP on the retained support, without histogram quantization. Require both classes for binary discrimination in this adapter: otherwise both metrics are null with an explicit reason, even though all-positive AP can conventionally be defined as1. Report selected, unknown, known, missing-prediction, scored-positive and scored-negative counts. No best-on-test threshold, model-selection rule or CI is supplied.

Depth support is explicit Z-by-column with integer absolute surface layers and integer z_offset. Require every depth voxel valid for a max/centroid/peak over the supplied profile. Require the entire inclusive requested surface band present and valid for its mean. A complete band can be valid within an incomplete wider profile; masks are distinct. Count excluded columns, zero-mass profiles and tied peaks. A zero profile has a valid maximum0 but no centroid or peak localization; a positive flat profile has a centroid but an ambiguous peak. Return NaN only in masked numerical arrays; JSON summaries must contain no NaN/Infinity. Report layer offsets only, never infer micrometres or a physical sign convention.

Use a ratio of mean in-band declared-clear to declared-ink probability only as a descriptive surface-response diagnostic. It needs both classes, complete valid bands and positive ink denominator; otherwise return null with reason. Do not clip ratios above1. Parent-group folds must exclude an entire declared group; the conservative six-fragment ledger yields four groups, and four ordinary leave-one-fragment-out folds retain a same-group sibling in training. This is a grouping audit, not six-data eligibility or a proof of common physical support.
