# Frozen structural-correlation diagnostic

Freeze before producing correlation scores or viewing CT. All six geometry-selected pairs remain included, with no replacement. This diagnostic measures structural correspondence, not ink identity. No teacher or label image participates.

Construct a32³ old-voxel lattice centered at the selected old surface point, offsets[-16,15]. Map it to modern coordinates with the published3×4 matrix. Blur modern native CT with Gaussian sigma=(7.91/2.4)/2 fine voxels, truncate3, then trilinearly sample onto that lattice. This is one specified coarse-observation surrogate, not a calibrated simulation of all scanner physics. Require complete sampled support with at least6fine voxels from raw block edges. Sample the real old CT on a48³ lattice offsets[-24,23], leaving an integer translation search of±8coarse voxels.

On each lattice use the local intensity minus Gaussian sigma1,truncate2 (in coarse-lattice units) as structural contrast. No normalization is fitted on labels. Define eight6³ support cubes: each coordinate index in[5,11) or[21,27) of the32³ template. Four cubes with even corner-parity form the fit mask; the four odd cubes are held-out checks. The gaps protect local filter support, including the mapped fine blur. These are fixed spatial supports, not detected or manually verified landmarks.

Fit one translation by maximizing normalized cross-correlation on the fit mask over integer shifts[-8,+8]³. Refine each coordinate only by a bounded±.5pixel quadratic interpolation of its immediate neighboring correlation scores. No rotation, affine, deformable or sign-inversion search. Independently find the best translation for each held-out support with the same search. Retain the complete correlation cubes and report peak correlation, second peak outside a3pixel Chebyshev neighborhood, boundary status and displacement. This small-patch proxy may be ambiguous or follow the wrong wrap; it does not automatically meet the physical-landmark requirement.

The strict numerical screen requires all four held-out peaks to be interior, NCC>=.5, competing-peak margin>=.05, tangential residual median<=.5coarse pixel and maximum<=1coarse pixel relative to the fitted translation. Also report normal residuals; actual neighboring-wrap separation/identity still requires image review. All six pairs must qualify for the planned balanced panel. Do not discard a failing pair and call the remainder a prespecified complete panel.

Render native cross-sections and surface-oriented intensity views using geometry-derived axes, showing native2.4µm CT, the fixed blurred surrogate and real7.91µm CT under the published mapping. Keep full acquired volumes. Review uncertainty explicitly; apparent structures or high structural correlations cannot create independent ink/background labels. If no defensible positive and negative reference support emerges, stop the ink-learning arms under the main protocol, regardless of correlations.
