# Next task: a reference-first native/synthetic positive control

Phase42 establishes that the paired scans can be sampled economically and that they share visible large-scale structure. It does not establish reference ink labels or sufficiently precise local real-scan correspondence. Stop this six-center panel; do not add chunks, alter its centers or weaken its admission thresholds. The acquisition-quality hypothesis remains open.

## Recommended change in order

Start from a documented visible-ink target with physical coordinates and defensible non-ink papyrus, then select CT. The six geometry-selected centers did not provide that reference. Use a publicly released expert annotation with its acquisition/coordinates and ancestry, or a small specifically reviewed annotation packet. Published figure text or a model output alone is insufficient. A request should ask for the exact volume, mesh/surface version, region coordinates, annotation basis and uncertainty—not a general provenance history.

The first route should be a localized visible-ink example underlying the modern high-resolution unwrapping work, if its coordinates and independently supported labels are available. This is a candidate to investigate, not an assertion that such a complete release exists. The retained topography paper says samples, labels and splits “will be released”; that statement is not a verified available dataset. Do not substitute it for actual access.

For a new candidate, inspect at most two documented sources/targets in one bounded reference-admission exercise. Require a concrete coordinate/annotation packet before any new CT acquisition. If neither yields one, report the missing packet and prepare a specific annotation/collaboration request; do not restart a general metadata search. Sending that request requires explicit user authorization. No new acquisition or training is authorized by this draft alone.

## Defensible supervision

An accepted reference must distinguish directly supported ink positives, non-ink on the same writing surface, and unknown material. Exclude air, neighboring wraps, ambiguous bright inclusions and uncertain boundaries. Record who annotated, what observation supported the judgment, coordinate mapping, confidence and any model assistance. Native-CT manual labels are also observation-dependent and require qualified review; they are not automatically chemical ground truth. If an optical photograph supplies labels, local optical-to-CT correspondence becomes a prerequisite for those labels.

Public transferred Paris4 dense labels can be described as weak or pseudo supervision. They cannot by themselves supply independent test truth for a descendant method. A native teacher's predictions likewise cannot be both training signal and the independent evidence that distillation worked.

## First learning comparison after reference admission

Use a conventional cleanly initialized native-input learner as the positive control, and a capacity-, supervision- and optimization-budget-matched learner trained on the synthetically degraded native observation. Generate degradation from the same native samples, so this arm has exact synthetic correspondence and does not wait for real-scan registration. Keep full approved depth support, physical block separation and future receptive-field/degradation guard bands. Freeze reference masks and physical splits before fitting; any reviewed region is disclosed as exposed rather than called an untouched test. A new freeze must define architecture, adequate CPU updates, fit checks, degradation, physical split, metrics and stopping rules before model outputs.

Train at both observation qualities and test within quality and across quality. That distinguishes a training-distribution mismatch from loss of task information better than testing a native-trained model only on degraded input. Real coarse CT joins this matrix only when its own structural correspondence and wrap identity are verified. A single Gaussian blur does not emulate differences in energy, phase contrast, reconstruction or noise; synthetic results remain conditional on the degradation used.

Stop if the native arm cannot learn defensible labels on training support under an adequate frozen budget; then diagnose reference, representation or optimization without invoking coarse acquisition as the explanation. If native training fits but separate-region transfer fails, prioritize coverage/representation over resolution or distillation. If native transfers while matched-quality synthetic-coarse remains substantially worse, proceed to training-only native-teacher supervision. If matched-quality synthetic-coarse recovers the deficit, prioritize acquisition-matched training first. A separate real-coarse deficit after registration would motivate scanner-domain modeling. These are decision branches, not observed phase42 outcomes.

Preserve the established +0.01 AP and regional-regression advancement rules for comparisons where that existing development evaluation applies. A new reference panel requires its own preregistered practical margin and physical-block uncertainty analysis; do not silently transplant an aggregate number into a different population or claim an independent improvement from already inspected Paris regions.

## What changes the present decision

A verified coordinate/annotation packet, with independent positive and non-ink support across physically separated regions, changes native/synthetic learning from inadmissible to a study that can be frozen. More correlation, longer adapter training, or a cleaner-looking prediction does not. Real-scan learning additionally needs held-out physical correspondences within the existing tolerance and bounded normal/wrap uncertainty. GPU remains deferred; CPU monetary spend is unrestricted and the first GPU pilot remains capped at £10 total.
