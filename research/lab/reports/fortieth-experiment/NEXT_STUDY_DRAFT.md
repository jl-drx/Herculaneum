# Proposed next study: isolate the new-crop supervised gradient

Phase40 is complete. Crop2 has the highest Paris AP among the five fixed references (0.317591101), only 0.000247059 above A512, and passes all regional retention limits. It misses the advancement gain gate. It also narrowly misses the all-three descriptive midpoint criterion: its worst AP delta is 0.000206010 worse, while aggregate AP and worst AUROC retention improve. Do not interpret that near miss as evidence that adding training data generally fails. Both mixed crops avoid A512's regional breach, but neither supports promotion or GPU expenditure.

The useful unresolved mechanism is whether the added crop's supervised BCE gradient contributes to this tradeoff, or whether reduced original-source supervision combined with preservation on the new inputs suffices. Before another crop search, coefficient sweep or representation change, recommend one trained control on the exact phase40 batches.

Reuse phase40's frozen crop2 input arrays, participation/class-selected masks, augmentations, original seed42 initialization, teacher cache, 512 updates and AdamW settings. Keep the original patch's BCE contribution at its existing joint-mask denominator; remove only the replacement patch's BCE gradient. Keep KL on both selected patches with exactly the same denominator and coefficient. In symbols, with N the original joint supported count, replace `(sum BCE_original + sum BCE_new)/N + KL_joint` by `sum BCE_original/N + KL_joint`. Do not renormalize the original patch's BCE or change the learning rate to compensate. Original-source exposure and both-input preservation then match phase40; only the new-source supervised-gradient term differs.

This is a post-result mechanistic proposal, not a pre-registered phase40 extension. The control is not label-free: its support and class balancing were selected using the supplied new-crop labels. It isolates their BCE-gradient contribution conditional on that fixed selection, not all annotation information or a pure diversity mechanism. Frozen teacher predictions are regularization targets, not new truth.

Baseline is the completed full phase40 mixture D512; retain seed42/A512/crop1/midpoint as references. Before execution, test that the gradient difference is exactly the removed new-crop BCE contribution and that original-patch BCE scaling and joint KL are unchanged. Report both the actually optimized control loss and the unchanged full phase40 objective on fixed support, so a mechanically smaller objective is not reported as improved fitting. Use the same eight reused development regions and the same advancement gate (+0.01 Paris AP versus seed42 and A512; no region AP/AUROC loss worse than -0.02).

Stop at 512 updates or an integrity/nonfinite/resource failure, with one endpoint and no extension, LR search or checkpoint selection. No new data acquisition or GPU is needed. Freeze new code, counts, verification and resource limits before model calls.

Separately compare Paris AP and worst regional AP/AUROC deltas versus seed42 with D512. If the control is no worse in all three, the new-crop BCE gradient is unnecessary for this observed tradeoff; prioritise objective/preservation balance over claiming a supervised-diversity benefit. If D512 is no worse in all three and strictly better in at least one, it supports a contribution from that gradient under this fixed design. Otherwise report a tradeoff, not an unqualified winner. Keep effect sizes visible; tiny descriptive differences or reused-region dominance do not establish statistical significance, accuracy or readiness for GPU work.

This proposal has not run. Seed42 remains the baseline, all 17 planes remain in use, and GPU work remains deferred.
