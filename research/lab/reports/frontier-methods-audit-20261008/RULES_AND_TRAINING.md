## Verified ink contributions and compatibility

The official [August Progress announcement](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/e0bbb8b40a2db58b1d71864f286eb85717e59e64/scrollprize.org/docs/15_winners.md#L57) names **Domenico Russo** ($1,000), **Paulo Sergio Camillo** ($1,000), and **Erwin Nieuwlaar** ($500). “Eriwn” is a spelling error. These amounts are awards, not compute prices. Author repositories/model cards establish the method details below.

Our target is **native 8.64 µm / 116 keV**, with 31 genuine rendered depth planes and a fixed centered 17-plane model window. Pooled 9.596 µm reference data is **11.06% coarser relative to target spacing**; Nieuwlaar's native 9.362 µm domain is **8.36% coarser**. Energy, native-versus-pooled statistics, plane centers and physical depth spans also differ. Similar voxel sizes do not prove transfer. No fitted resampling or depth-sign search is justified by this audit.

### Russo: dense supervision, useful matched ablation, corrected leakage

[Code pin `dd5bc8fb300c810c6ec042cb7e068be78829d086`](https://github.com/DomRusso2/ink9um-dense/tree/dd5bc8fb300c810c6ec042cb7e068be78829d086); [weights pin `3e5e7ed44e04606cf4a3766cf83a016543a30d83`](https://huggingface.co/domenicor046/ink9um-dense/tree/3e5e7ed44e04606cf4a3766cf83a016543a30d83); [pseudo-label pin `185a80ae4b9e75417bbb831797e6ab9e9e836fbc`](https://huggingface.co/domenicor046/ink9um-dense-labels/tree/185a80ae4b9e75417bbb831797e6ab9e9e836fbc). Author identity is stated in the README; the official Russo award entry itself has no author link.

Canonical 2.4 µm teacher predictions densify seven pooled 9.596 µm segments; per-segment thresholds are calibrated on manual supervision, and manual labels override teacher targets. Input is **17 of 21 planes**, jitter ±2, robust-MAD normalization, BCE smoothing 0.5, batch 64, seed 42. The matched control/all-seven/ex016 configurations retain the same 29 representations and physical IDs. **“w016 excluded” excludes its dense labels, not the segment:** w016 remains sparsely, manually supervised. Unsupervised zeros are not negatives.

The August 18 corrected masks place **2,924 w016 pixels** previously used by all-seven pseudo training into validation, about 1.6% of that validation domain. Recommended ex016, manual control and released baseline checkpoints are reportedly unaffected by that particular correction. There is also a declared **w028 aligned / w044 native physical alias** in the released split. These are concrete reasons to version masks and audit physical support, not merely compare segment names.

Recommended ex016 versus control gains in reported balanced accuracy are **+0.0480 / +0.0790 / +0.0068** on the three validation regions; the often quoted +0.077/+0.100/+0.026 belongs to all-seven. On PHerc1667/w029, ex016 AUC **0.8853** is lower than control **0.8945** despite its small balanced-accuracy gain. The all-seven model merges visible strokes on w016 while scoring better at threshold 0.5. Only PHerc0814 exceeds the best of fourteen released checkpoints in the reported comparison. Checkpoint/seed selection and visual recommendation use these same few regions, so they are development evidence, not untouched test results.

Four ~138 MB checkpoints are publicly registered; recommended `dense9um-w016excluded-step075000.pth` has published SHA-256 **`1d7b99bc641b459736a762b2a1d8c357f61ada4f0117c24c7327b6a1293ba1eb`**, **138,367,629 bytes**, and the advertised model/config/step format. This makes it the simpler **first contract candidate**, not an approved plug-in. Training specifies 78,125 steps, while the released final comparison uses the last regular **75,000-step** save. The repository lacks vendored trainer/teacher implementations and a dependency lock; scoring/fetch helpers import missing modules and use Windows paths. Author runtime is **9.5 hours on one 16 GB RTX 5070 Ti**, ~8.6 GB training VRAM, Torch 2.11/cu128; these are reported measurements, not our resource estimate. See [detailed source audit](source-evidence/official-contributions/RUSSO_AUDIT.md).

### Nieuwlaar: native-domain hypothesis; verify an unresolved weight contract

[Code pin `217e7521670db49cde97872ea6a8bdfb80c449f1`](https://github.com/Nieuwlaar/ink9um-dense-native/tree/217e7521670db49cde97872ea6a8bdfb80c449f1); [model pin `b894a0493ab2291cc8dc3247020c0d7cb0967a58`](https://huggingface.co/Nieuwlaar/ink9um-dense-native/tree/b894a0493ab2291cc8dc3247020c0d7cb0967a58). Reproduction names upstream `koine_machines` commit **`3ea17f54a9b3d5fd1aaf73e1d2c8386dbaa9f30e`** and a Torch 2.4.1/CUDA 12.1 image. HF supplies source tables missing from the GitHub tree.

The treatment adds dense teacher targets on **ten PHerc0139 native 9.362 µm / 113 keV segments**, compared with a matched control, initialized from Russo ex016. Native inputs contain **28 planes, annotation at 14**; pooled inputs have 21 planes, annotation at 10. Both use a jittered 17-plane hybrid recipe. Label generation combines a registered 78 keV teacher with manual threshold calibration. Native supervision requires observed-intensity validity **and teacher > 0**; manual target replacement does not automatically add manual pixels to that supervision mask. Preserve explicit annotation/geometry/observation masks when adapting this recipe; intensity validity is not physical papyrus truth.

The Title/w024/w047/w053 exclusions are name-based; full physical support independence across teachers, aliases and scan energies is not established. Reported AUC gains use **model maps as references**, mainly the teacher's 78 keV family. A separate 59 keV model reference is still a model, not authentic ground truth. Step 16,000 is selected using benchmark results. Improved teacher agreement and a student-cloning ratio do not prove recovered ink.

Registered `dense_native_016000.safetensors` and `soup42_last4.safetensors` are each **272,767,904 bytes**; documentation reports **68,175,426 parameters**, whereas our instantiated baseline has **34,546,498**. This is an **unresolved architecture/serialization contract inconsistency**, not a verified compatible state dict. No weight bytes were fetched to resolve it. Require pinned architecture and strict tensor-name/shape verification; never use partial loading to hide the mismatch. The published dense weight SHA is **`d3d95dc41dcb24ed76b84b5e12ca5dae25d7fb34edf1dfbb93e0186f91e6fc6d`**.

Low-training-cost leads are same-seed trajectory soup and shifted-depth **prediction** averaging. The author warns against weight soup across seeds; combine predictions instead. Native shell ranges of 19–20 input planes conflict with its “17-slice” comment, so resolve exact inference semantics before translating them to our 31-plane source. Preserve raw scores rather than treating the published display rescale as confidence. A **26 August correction** reports that raw-darkness vetoing erased 50–65% of known ink; it is now report-only unless enabled. Do not adopt it as an ink test. The separately repaired Scroll 1 ResNet50 belongs to a different 7.9 µm lineage.

Author cost reports (~five hours on two cloud RTX 4090s, ~$4 compute) are not a current price quote or an independently reproduced economic result. The recipe stages roughly **250 GB** of source inputs; source transfer/storage can dominate. A checkpoint's existence alone is insufficient completion evidence. Model-card MIT does not settle upstream label/data rights.

### Camillo: depth width and jitter require a retraining experiment

[Code pin `999b912c1ce92b8f5ab63ef2cc90ea017dc6ad04`](https://github.com/pscamillo/ink9-depth-window/tree/999b912c1ce92b8f5ab63ef2cc90ea017dc6ad04). Matched **17/13/9/5-plane retraining** on pooled 9.596 µm inputs tests width × jitter, not truncation of an existing 17-plane model. Author blind judgments give 60 ties for 13/9 versus 17; five planes with ±2 jitter mostly loses, while five with zero jitter ties visually but loses **0.133 balanced accuracy on w029**. One seed, one judge and training-segment windows limit interpretation; three window pairs overlap. Missing baseline configuration, weights, historical trainer pin and locks prevent a complete reproduction. Actual default window conversion discards the shipped English keys, and its integrity check can pass with no predictions. These need fixing before reuse.

RTX 5070 throughput/runtime claims (5.20 versus 3.24 iterations/s; 4h10 versus 6h06) are author reports without retained memory/cost logs. MIT code does not relicense checkpoint/data sources. This is a later efficiency ablation after the supervision benchmark, not the first experiment. See [detailed audit](source-evidence/official-contributions/CAMILLO_AUDIT.md).

### Socratic Method: a separate surface-model track

[Pinned Socratic repository](https://github.com/Hob3rMallow/socratic_method/tree/7f86cbf8d38155b37fdd39449c6775ec50344b6c) is the ScrollFiesta team's **volumetric papyrus-surface predictor**, not an ink detector. F0's corpus lists PHerc0800 as unseen, registers our volume and accepts 8.64 µm, but upstream teacher/M7 lineage remains incompletely audited. Its 689-row benchmark uses hardened teacher labels and also selects checkpoint/threshold. Native CT context, CUDA/BF16, different model/normalization and geometry outputs are required. It cannot consume our flattened stack as an ink model. See [geometry audit](GEOMETRY_AND_SURFACES.md) for exact weight identity, resource limits, licensing and a conditional pilot triggered by a measured geometry problem.

## A feasible next benchmark and stopping rules

1. **Verify one candidate contract on CPU.** Start with Russo's recommended 75k checkpoint metadata and pinned config; compare names/shapes, preprocessing, centered depth, normalization and raw output semantics against our instantiated adapter before any separately budgeted weight retrieval. Nieuwlaar requires resolution of the contract mismatch first. Do not select checkpoints on target letter resemblance.
2. **Audit a small corrected public validation region before predictions.** PHerc1667/w029 has a supplied validation mask and verified current public surface identity `20251212185248-w029_20251212185248662_flatboi`, volume `20251217075048`. It is a pooled ~9.596 µm candidate, not the unresolved paper `l_2/l_5` aliases. Freeze at most a 256² annotation-selected crop after verifying aligned-source version, corrected ink labels, supervision and validation masks, depth mapping and chunk budget. Its source-native 109 planes must not be silently treated as the curated centered 21-plane representation. Keep the existing w013 training-overlapped control only as a pipeline check.
3. **Make lineage and support explicit.** Evaluation requires supplied annotation validity and geometry/observed support, not `label == 0` as universal background. Separate genuine manual, iterative/model-assisted and teacher targets. Record teacher/student source lists, old/new masks, all 3D interpolation corners/depths and model-context halos across aliases. Region-withheld evaluation inside a trained segment is not segment-held-out; missing physical/teacher independence means an **in-distribution masked comparison**, not authentic generalization. If genuine independent ink truth is unavailable, label the result teacher/annotation agreement and stop accuracy claims.
4. **Run existing fixed seeds first, then one verified candidate.** Same bytes, support and physical units; retain raw floating scores. Report tie-aware ROC AUC, precision–recall and a threshold fixed using separate development data, plus source images and blank/positive controls. Use physical regions as sampling units; a tiny crop gives descriptive results, not pixel-count-based certainty. A predefined ensemble is a separate arm. Do not report balanced-accuracy gains alone as legibility or optimize target appearance.
5. **Measure economics before training.** A future short GPU pilot should time a frozen workload after warm-up, measure VRAM/RAM and throughput, and include staging, startup, checkpoint/result I/O, billed time and shutdown failures. It must fit the separately approved aggregate cost cap. A short inference pilot does not reproduce a 5–10-hour training claim. Only then test dense native-domain supervision against a matched control on physically separated genuine reference support.

Stop adoption/training on unresolved source identity, label permissions or lineage; unknown physical split; architecture mismatch; missing essential code/config; invalid/unobserved source support; raw-darkness assumptions; only teacher-label improvement; worse authentic positive/negative controls; or inability to fit measured data/time/cost bounds. Current PHerc0800 responses provide **no defensible letter sequence** and must not become pseudo-training truth merely because a detector produces blobs. Target eligibility, technical compatibility and prize compliance remain three separate questions.
