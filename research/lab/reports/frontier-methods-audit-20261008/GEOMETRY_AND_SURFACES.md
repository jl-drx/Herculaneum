# Frontier geometry and surface methods audit — 8 October 2026

**Recommendation: keep the fifth study intact and hold geometry-model adoption until a measured geometry error explains a bottleneck.** The three requested methods concern papyrus surfaces, patch connectivity and unwrapping. They do not replace the ink detector or establish a reading. Recovering rendered samples from native CT verifies a sampling implementation; it does not prove correct wrap identity, recto placement or papyrus separation.

This is a primary-source audit, not a reproduction of author results. It retained **2,678,882 response-body bytes across 74 requests**, below the shared 10 MiB cap. No scans, weights, dependencies, builds, training or provider jobs were downloaded or run. `network-ledger.json` records URLs, retrieval times, status, exact bytes and SHA-256; each raw body has adjacent metadata. Two capped responses are marked incomplete and are not used as complete commit/tree evidence. Full replacement commit metadata and focused directory trees support the conclusions below. The current official winners page is retained; the collaborating rules audit also supplied official winners Markdown pinned at Villa `e0bbb8b40a2db58b1d71864f286eb85717e59e64`.

## William Stevens: patch consistency before flattening

The [pinned pipeline9 documentation](https://github.com/WillStevens/scrollreading/blob/62cbc21bdafbe0313fee11d781d729256a14262e/pipeline9/readme.md) takes full-resolution **3D surface-prediction Zarr `/0`** and quarter-resolution CT `/2`. It grows/caches a vector field, generates patch binaries and relationships, excludes inconsistent patches, refines 2D placements and anneals components. Its five-float patch records contain local quad X/Y and native CT X/Y/Z. These are different inputs from our flattened `[31,H,W]` stacks.

The implementation compares 3D disagreement between overlapping 2D patch cells using normal projections. `badpatchfinder.cpp` defaults to a **10-native-voxel** conflict cutoff. `ScorePlacementAreaAndIncon` rewards coarse 2D occupancy and subtracts normal-projected disagreement; multi-component annealing uses a **30-voxel** scale. Sampling is on a fixed five-quad grid. This score is neither a measured sheet-switch error rate nor independently verified physical coverage. Missing normals can become zero and hide disagreement. Exported weighted-average coordinates must be reviewed together with conflict masks.

The principal build is CPU C++/OpenMP with blosc2 and libtiff, under **GPL-3.0**. The documentation suggests 16–32 GB for whole-scroll runs, without a measured small-region resource benchmark. Annealing seeds `mt19937` from `random_device`; the named `RANDOM_SEED` constant does not make those runs repeatable. The stock surface reader expects uint8, **192³ Blosc/Zstd chunks**, fixed caches and local files; missing files become zeros. It cannot safely receive an arbitrary Zarr source without a declared input/support bridge.

Stock parameters target PHerc0139 at 9 µm, with four native voxels per quad step. PHerc0800 requires explicit 8.64 µm units: quad spacing 34.56 µm, and the stock 10/30-voxel scales correspond to 86.4/259.2 µm. The tifxyz exporter changes mesh scale to `[0.25,0.25]`; preserving this conversion is essential. The complete pinned tree contains no award-run patch binaries, relationship tables, output volumes or weights, and no release assets supply them. The 11,006,888-byte report12 PDF was outside the bounded audit. Thus the official **365 cm² statement remains reported coverage, not a reproduced result**. Detailed code evidence and source integrity checks are in `raw-sources/william/notes.md`.

## ScrollFiesta: current geometry pipeline and released limitations

The [pinned README](https://github.com/Hob3rMallow/scrollfiesta_public/blob/bce8a65f97652316efb084171a17d2f1f8d3ae1a/README.md) explicitly calls this the geometry half of the work. Its ordinary path is `grid_pipeline → scroll_whole → scroll_unroll`; September adds `sheet_assemble`, then CT baking, `sheet_layers` and `vmesh_tifxyz`. Inputs are matching 128³ uint8 prediction-mask and RAW CT TIFF cubes in native coordinates. Outputs include mesh/seam audits, flattened sheets, CT textures, masks and geometry metrics. `sheet_layers` produces layers along the inward normal for a later ink detector; it does not detect ink.

The default path runs on CPU. Optional CubeCL CUDA/HIP projection accelerates one hotspot. Portable builds require CMake ≥3.24, C17/C++17, TIFF and BLAS/LAPACK; missing LAPACK can skip the whole-scroll/assembly tools. Large Linux runs are explicitly not validated in the current assembly documentation. Public API meshes use XYZ, while CLI OBJ columns follow internal ZYX order; an unnoticed axis or winding conversion would invalidate our CT comparison.

The public configuration is calibrated to **PHerc0139**, with its own axis table, eight threads and a 900-second repair budget. It is not a PHerc0800 preset. The [September public reference check](https://github.com/Hob3rMallow/scrollfiesta_public/blob/bce8a65f97652316efb084171a17d2f1f8d3ae1a/submission_update/release/README.md) on a saved 100-cube pile reports **47.385% source-area coverage and 171 overlapping triangle pairs**, with `geometry_qualified=false`. A successful build, tests or zero exit status does not change that verdict. The saved mesh/CT pile is outside Git, and the result resumed a retained placement; fresh runs can vary with scheduling and timing. The curated August exhibits use different workflows: notably, the 21³ result is stage-1 concatenation, **not a completed global fit**.

Original code is MIT, but the [dependency inventory](https://github.com/Hob3rMallow/scrollfiesta_public/blob/bce8a65f97652316efb084171a17d2f1f8d3ae1a/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md) identifies research-only GCoptimization, restricted Triangle redistribution and additional unresolved/preserved notices. Calling the complete binary unconditionally MIT would be inaccurate. This audit reads those terms without accepting or redistributing anything.

## Socratic F0: the actual 9 µm surface release

The [current model record](https://github.com/Hob3rMallow/socratic_method/blob/7f86cbf8d38155b37fdd39449c6775ec50344b6c/CURRENT_MODEL.json) identifies **C3-F0, selected at 200,000 sample exposures after a complete 250,000-exposure run**. Weights date from 7 September; shipped inference was promoted on 9 September: eight-way mirror TTA, threshold **0.30**, unchanged weights. The LFS pointer and recipe agree on **409,674,095 bytes** and SHA-256 `54db9a59cb602ff08e3dda0acc1195ad980ab05fb660332d32fb436a0d49a175`. This is a published artifact identity; weight contents were not fetched or independently verified. Hugging Face files in the repo include templates with placeholders, so they do not establish a separately published model mirror.

It is a **102,349,770-parameter residual-encoder 3D nnU-Net**, initialized from released M7. Input is one native CT channel; output is two-class logits and foreground softmax, not text or ink probability. The recipe clips uint8 CT to `[0,212]` and normalizes with mean 87.54424285888672 and standard deviation 47.74376678466797. Each target cube is 128³ with a 32-voxel halo, 192³ context, Gaussian sliding-window blending and BF16 CUDA. The runner stages all 26 neighboring RAW cubes and skips targets missing real context. It writes binary sparse OME-Zarr plus float16 probability TIFFs and ScrollFiesta inputs; sparse zero-fill must not be mistaken for observed background.

The package requires Python `>=3.11,<3.14`; the observed run used Python 3.12.9, Torch 2.11.0+cu128, nnUNet 2.8.1 and dynamic-network-architectures 0.4.4. The shipped runner pins the **CUDA device type** and queries the selected GPU's power limit, refusing values above **600.5 W**. This is a power-limit check, not a universal prohibition on every GPU bearing an H100 name. No GPU memory fit was benchmarked here. The author gives roughly one second per cube with TTA on an RTX PRO 6000; that excludes our unmeasured provisioning, staging and memory costs. Original code and released weights are Apache-2.0; upstream scans, M7 and fine-teacher artifacts retain their own terms.

The wide15 corpus records 15,432 training rows over twelve scrolls, combining fine-teacher soft targets and human hard labels. PHerc0800 is explicitly marked **unseen by F0's listed corpus**, its native volume `20250521135224` is registered, and 8.64 µm lies inside the accepted 7.5–10 µm range. That makes a bounded native-CT trial technically relevant; it does not establish PHerc0800 accuracy or complete upstream-model independence.

The September benchmark reports macro Dice **0.7130** and composite **0.6521** on 689 rows from PHerc0814/PHerc1451. Labels are registered **fine-teacher predictions hardened at q≥0.5**, not human truth. Of these rows, 442 are real coarse-volume crops and 247 are downsampled fine scans. The same validation harness selects the late checkpoint and the inference threshold, even under a preregistered selection rule; its result is not untouched test accuracy. The human-labelled PHerc0500P2 comparison is not independent of M7's training lineage. Random-cube “solid mass” measurements are proxies without ground truth and cannot see fused wraps where CT has no discernible gap. These limits are disclosed by the authors and must survive any summary of their gains. Repair/continuity/patch postprocessors remain qualified at **0.35**, not the shipped 0.30; enabling them would be a separate change.

## Two bounded next reproductions, with decision gates

**1. CPU patch-conflict preflight, followed by a geometry-bottleneck decision.** Freeze two real 256² XYZ extents inside the verified fourth field: A `[1024,384,256,256]`, B `[1152,512,256,256]`; sample every fourth pixel, preserving their known 128² overlap. A third patch is an explicitly synthetic copy of B displaced 40 native voxels along a frozen valid reference normal, with its incorrect relationship declared before scoring. Use the focused pinned Stevens conflict routine with a declared adapter and normal/coordinate conventions, not whole-scroll growth or unseeded annealing. The three five-float patch binaries total 245,760 bytes; include normals and relationship records under a **1 MiB input cap**, **2 GiB memory**, four threads and **60 seconds**. Metrics: compatible-pair normal-projected disagreement, injected-conflict detection, excluded patch IDs, normal degeneracy, unique retained source support and exact coordinate/export round trip. Run A/B alone as the compatible control; do not presume which member a greedy three-patch rejection will discard. Stop on units/normal failure, rejection of A/B alone, missed injected conflict or resource breach. This validates a code/data bridge using real geometry and a synthetic fault; it does **not** demonstrate correct papyrus topology.

Before moving from that preflight to a new geometry model, inspect fixed native-CT cross-sections with model maps hidden and record uncertain surface/face assignments. Predeclare coarse-voxel normal placement error, confirmed wrong-sheet assignments and ambiguous cases. A concrete engineering trigger is median absolute normal error above **two native voxels**, or a confirmed wrong-sheet assignment, on a fixed annotated diagnostic set. That tolerance is a proposed engineering gate, not a biological ground-truth rule. If geometry cannot be distinguished in the native CT, record it as unresolved; if no bottleneck is measured, **hold new meshing/surface models and continue the present interpretation controls**.

**2. Conditional Socratic-to-mesh ROI pilot, without training.** If a geometry bottleneck is confirmed, freeze one native 3×3×3 target-cube block, selecting coordinates from geometry/coverage rather than attractive predictions, and analyze its central cube with real neighboring support. Predicting those 27 cubes stages at most 125 native 128³ RAW cubes: **250 MiB of staged uint8 CT**, before source-storage chunk overhead. Pin actual remote chunk contracts and wire caps separately. Cap the pilot at **five GPU minutes**, **2 GiB retained outputs**, and the already approved aggregate **£10 ceiling**, subject to a pinned compatible image, verified account, quoted price and independent shutdown. Do not infer hardware memory fit from that bound. Use F0/200k, TTA and 0.30 unchanged; compare with published M7 at its documented setting on the exact same native CT, then mesh only with independently pinned axes and support. Keep opt-in repairs off.

Freeze assessor-blind coarse-CT surface annotations before inspecting the new maps. Primary metrics should be surface boundary agreement at a declared voxel tolerance and confirmed false sheet connections; secondary metrics are retained supported area, invalid/overlap faces, normal placement error, peak memory, source bytes and seconds per cube. Retain every selected case and failed stage. Existing fifth-field/peak-centered cases are post hoc diagnostics; a spatial control must be declared separately, and disjointness must use complete CT/render support rather than base-point distance alone. If annotations or complete support are unavailable, label the run only as an implementation reproduction and stop short of an accuracy/improvement claim. Abort on hash/axis/normalization mismatch, missing context, resource/cost breach or an unqualified geometry export. Freeze settings and splits before outcomes; do not tune thresholds or select the best-looking checkpoint on these cases. A positive pilot would justify a larger independently annotated geometry benchmark, **not ink training or a translated reading**.

The source audit supports studying these methods. It does not support adopting them on prize rank, applying them to our flattened stacks as if they were ink models, or treating their published benchmark gains as authenticated PHerc0800 text.
