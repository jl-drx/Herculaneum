# Return to the sealed-scroll objective

The user's criticism identifies a real design problem: technically correct, tightly bounded studies were becoming prerequisites to stronger experiments without establishing their ability to detect a useful effect. We should retain their observations, narrow their negative conclusions, and stop extending the same small training distribution. The approved 8,192-update native profilometry run closes that branch. Further profilometry studies and the unsent Paris4 reference request are shelved.

## PHerc343 is the immediate positive-control route

[Nieuwlaar's repository](https://github.com/Nieuwlaar/pherc343-first-letters/tree/e8370f1f88a211166056df62ea6fcfffe4a1063e) is pinned at `e8370f1f88a211166056df62ea6fcfffe4a1063e`. It identifies PHerc0343 scan `20250509220833-8.640um-1.2m-116keV` and masked volume `20250521140437`. PHerc0800's retained catalog identifies the same voxel size and X-ray energy; that is a relevant acquisition match, not proof of identical reconstruction or material properties.

The recipe uses m7-snapped spiral meshes, a 66-layer VC3D render, dense_native/16k, official seeds42/43 at75k and Hecate9.6 µm. It sweeps all four models at 31 depth centres in both directions, repeats inference with a shifted tile phase, merges the two phases by mesh ownership, and forms an ensemble from per-model z-scores. The published image uses d=+1 in the reading direction. The full recipe is **496 maps on a 2560×6720 canvas**, with an author estimate of roughly six RTX4090 hours for ink inference. A small crop reproduction is not a full-recipe runtime estimate or reproduction.

Important physical conventions:

- VC3D layer k lies at k−32.5 native voxels along the mesh normal. The mesh lies between layers32 and33. The author's named depth d=k−33 is a display/index convention.
- Villa models take 17 native planes centred at layer33+d. Hecate takes 16 planes at 9.6 µm, centred at native layer32.5+d. These centres differ by half a native voxel in the published recipe. Preserve and report it before trying a matched-physical-centre ablation.
- Hecate preparation resamples **both XY and depth**: XY factor8.64/9.6=0.9 and depth step9.6/8.64=1.111… native layers. Merely declaring 9.6 µm to the CLI does not resample.
- Both directions are evaluated. PHerc343's chosen reverse/reading convention does not automatically determine which side of PHerc0800 carries text.
- Snap uses the nearest binary predicted-sheet run, then rejects neighbourhood jumps, fills and smooths offsets. It can still choose the wrong wrap. Do not impose it on all PHerc0800 geometry because it helped another scroll.
- Z-score display ensembles and clipped/quantized public TIFF scores are not calibrated ink probabilities. Keep raw single-model maps and all depth results alongside any display ensemble.

The author states that none of the four models trained on the PHerc343 volume. The released Hecate card does not enumerate a complete teacher/training ancestry, so that statement remains author-reported rather than an independently completed lineage audit. The user reports papyrologist readings. This turn verified the public recipe, published image SHA, and exact mesh concatenation; it did not obtain an independent specialist review record or reproduce CT rendering/ink inference. The official homepage's linked Substack page was blocked by the inherited proxy at CONNECT; the source-access limitation is retained. This is sufficient to adopt a **reported positive control for reproduction**, without turning missing pixel-level truth into another indefinite blocker.

The source boxes locate 18 letter candidates and guided the seam/row presentation. They contain no transcriptions and are not a pixel-level ink segmentation target. The published sidecar's whole image canvas is10.338cm², with6.4619cm² estimated mesh area. Its `max-cm2=12` rendering flag is not a prize-rule amendment. The current official homepage still describes ten letters within a single4 cm² window. A prize submission would need its own qualifying subregion and current-rule check; research can use the larger published image.

## Hecate changes the prior-art baseline

[Hecate](https://huggingface.co/scrollprize/hecate/tree/9cb86e500e944b11a06a7020403cde5dffb5bcb2) is a volumetric ink predictor with learned depth attention, derived from a canonical ResNet152 3D backbone, rather than the existing hybrid3D/2D model. It exposes 3D logits and a 2D projection, but is not a papyrus-surface predictor. It uses existing 2D labels, imperfect 3D teachers and selected student snapshots. Its coarse model was trained first on degraded fine CT and then on aligned real native coarse renders where alignment passed; failed pairs retained fine-derived inputs.

That directly overlaps the generic paired-acquisition distillation proposal. We should use Hecate as a strong released baseline and seek a contribution in uncertainty-aware pairing, geometry/adjacent-wrap control, transfer or credible evaluation. No novelty claim follows from introducing a fine teacher alone. Its intended middle-sheet localization is not guaranteed; learned 3D output does not authenticate the writing surface. The model card requires9.6 µm sampling in all three axes, uint8 unnormalized input, division by255, 16×64×64 patches, and strict EMA checkpoint loading. The pinned 9.6 µm artifact is522,576,086bytes, SHA256 `809f4f10f7cb7afa19b4bee0f7d2ab31edd7e11b664f9f210cc9c17f22fcfe5d`. No Hecate checkpoint was downloaded or executed in this study.

## PHerc0139 before another Paris4 alignment campaign

The retained official catalog and **11 successful live array-metadata reads** confirm fine78keV/2.399µm and native113keV/9.362µm surface volumes for the title, w024, w047 and w053. w024/w047/w053 also provide a59keV/1.129µm volume rendered at level1. These are real separate acquisitions with matching segment identifiers, transformed meshes and published volume transforms. They are not merely artificially pooled versions of one scan.

Nieuwlaar's native benchmark is immediately useful as a reproducible **reference-agreement benchmark**: confident classes from the 78 keV model map, explicit shift/rotation and reversed-depth controls, a second59keV model reference for w024, and an author-designated blank segment w053. Its native training recipe excludes these four segments. The harness resizes a fine model map to native dimensions; it does not independently measure local registration residual. Shared identifiers, transforms and resizing therefore establish a pairing mechanism but not verified fine/coarse tissue correspondence everywhere. Teacher-family agreement can inflate results; the 59 keV comparator and blank/shift controls must accompany the main score. A blank designation is also an evidence claim to retain, not chemical proof of no ink.

Prioritize a bounded geometry/CT correspondence check on these published pairs, then use them with explicit uncertainty masks. Do not train on the designated benchmark segments, use their reference values to fit thresholds, or count the already inspected Paris regions as new test material. Cross-energy effects mean any result concerns the acquisition bundle, not resolution alone.

## Replace the GPU decision rule

A GPU pilot may proceed when there is (1) a concrete matched hypothesis and credible measured reference, (2) enough genuinely varied training data and an exposure/learning-rate schedule justified by a released recipe or learning curves, (3) a frozen endpoint and practical effect/retention criteria, and (4) a measured throughput/memory/cost estimate within the £10 **total** cap, pinned CUDA environment, working artifact delivery and an independent termination mechanism. CPU AP improvement is not a universal prerequisite.

One 20k-step arm pair is a bounded pilot, not automatically a statistically powered study. Released checkpoint variability informs sensitivity, while paired initializations and multiple independent training seeds estimate treatment variability. Temporal checkpoints and correlated pixels do not supply independent replicates. Use paired regional effects and parent-level summaries; with only two Paris parents, uncertainty about transfer is intrinsically broad. A block bootstrap can describe spatial sampling variability conditional on those regions, but cannot manufacture between-parent or between-training-seed evidence. If budget only buys one arm pair, disclose that and require later replication before a frontier-performance claim.

The archived full-config diff also shows that the two released seed artifacts have different fixed-scroll-prior manifests, and seed43 explicitly stores some optimizer settings absent from seed42's serialized mapping. Omitted fields may resolve to the same defaults; absence is not proof of a different optimizer. Nevertheless these are released training outcomes, not a controlled new experiment changing initialization alone. `RELEASED_SEED_CONFIG_DIFFERENCES.json` preserves the exact differences. Calling their spread a pure initialization-noise estimate would overstate the evidence.

Keep +0.01 AP as a provisional practical effect size where meaningful, and report uncertainty separately. Do not make the target “twice a two-seed spread”: that can reject valuable paired effects or produce an unstable hurdle. Also do not retrospectively relax a past gate to declare a failed run successful. A new target-native benchmark may require a different predeclared primary endpoint (legibility/source correspondence or reference agreement), with the old Paris panel retained as a regression diagnostic.

The local Runpod credential-import and boot-time-delivery gaps remain operational work to finish, not scientific reasons to demand more weak CPU adaptations. No GPU was rented or billed in this study. Historical $4 compute does not settle today's total charge, tax, storage, currency margin or failed-start cost.

## Direction

Next: reproduce the PHerc343 positive control at a fixed published depth and orientation, including the physically correct Hecate preparation; then transfer the fixed recipe to a geometry-chosen PHerc0800 region with a 66-layer render and full±15centre sweep. Treat mesh snapping as a controlled factor where measured local disagreement warrants it. PHerc0139 is the supporting paired-acquisition benchmark and training-source route. Keep a prospective PHerc0800 region for assessment after fixing implementation and display settings; none of our old target or Paris crops is that reserve.

If PHerc0800 remains unrecovered despite a successful matched-acquisition control, publish the source-linked failure diagnostics. The organisers' [2026 open problems](https://scrollprize.org/2026_open_problems) explicitly distinguish absent ink signal from signal not yet exploitable because of geometry, labels, architecture or training data. A null detector output cannot settle absence. Our useful contribution would be a reproducible separation of those alternatives, not another binary “no ink” label.
