# Approved CPU budget × objective comparison

User approved this proposal after discussion on9October2026. This is one experiment with two matched full-network trajectories and fixed32/512-update comparisons, not a depth/fusion adapter test. All existing input planes in seed42's17-plane contract are retained; this does not mean the model sees every available native CT plane. No GPU, network, new acquisition, model promotion, publication or submission is part of the study.

## Inputs and objectives

Start both arms from the same original seed42 weights with fresh AdamW, learning rate1e-6, betas(.9,.999), epsilon1e-8, weight decay0, global gradient clipping1, no scheduler. Train all264 canonical tensors/34,546,498 parameters. CPUFP32 model, deterministic algorithms,4intraop/1interop threads; Python3.12.14,NumPy2.5.3,Torch2.12.0+cpu.

Use exactly the32 batch-two PHerc0139 inputs, XY augmentations, normalization, schedule and masks from phase13, repeated16times. No reshuffling or new augmentation between cycles. Repetition deliberately isolates optimisation duration from expanded data; exposures are highly dependent and do not represent new examples. Both arms retain the same17planes and original depth order. Each schedule cycle has54,701unique supervised pixels and618,208manual pixel visits. No new spatial split is claimed.

Manual participation M is supplied supervision∩acquired support∩not retired validation. Source annotation ancestry is incomplete; “manual” is operational, not authenticated human-only ink truth. Unknown/retired labels never become negatives or BCE/Dice targets. Historical seed43 arrays are read only by the unchanged batch-identity/diagnostic helper; their target losses are never optimized. Teacher regularization is detached original seed42 logits on M only, not independent truth. Current public geometry checks do not authenticate historical private tissue masks. No optical labels/images, Frag6 data, reserve-parent material or Paris labels enter gradients or teacher generation.

A_control reproduces phase13manual A: mean BCE on M with0/1 targets mapped to.25/.75, plus mean KL(seed42||student) on M, coefficient1. BCE is FP32; original numerically stable KL is FP64.

B_dice changes only addition of unit-weight masked soft Dice. Sigmoid probabilities, unsmoothed binary M targets, per-patch/channel spatial sums, smooth constant1, arithmetic mean across two patches, no batch pooling: `DiceLoss=-mean((2*sum_M(p*y)+1)/(sum_M(p)+sum_M(y)+1))`. This is the pinned upstream gradient-equivalent negative Dice convention; using1−Dice would add a constant only. Every patch has both supervised classes under the inherited support gate. Keep BCE smoothing and KL coefficient/support unchanged. This adds one upstream objective component, not the whole historical upstream recipe or an intrinsically optimized objective. It changes gradient shape/scale; conclusions are conditional on the fixed optimizer.

## Frozen measurements and compute

Each arm performs512updates without optimizer resets. Retain every preupdate logit map, batch identity, supported loss, gradient norm and zero unsupported-gradient check. Fixed risk is measured on all32schedule batches at completed updates32,128,256,384,512; update0 is the unchanged teacher-cache risk. These measurements must not change model, optimizer, gradients, modes or RNG. Equal-batch means preserve actual repeated exposures. Record each arm's optimized risk and common hard-label BCE, smoothed BCE, Dice, KL and teacher drift. Do not compare unlike total objective values as model quality.

Both arms receive a first-post-update forward. A must reproduce its retained phase12first-step state/logits and phase13update32semantic state plus all32fixed-risk logit maps exactly. A failure stops execution before a longer-duration claim. Save exact raw states at32and512 only, with safe reload/schema/alias/state checks; optimizer continues uninterrupted across32. No best checkpoint selection. Each arm:673batch-two forwards,512backwards/updates,0teacher forwards.

Seven pre-execution numerical fixtures check independent scalar Dice, finite-difference gradients, unknown-support exclusion, patch reduction, empty support, pinned upstream value/gradient equality and control/full-objective support identity. A separate NumPy checker reconstructs all training logit losses and fixed means, with absolute per-scalar tolerance1e-6 frozen before outputs. It does not replay neural autograd or optimizer updates.

After training numerical verification, evaluate both arms at32and512 on all six retained Paris regions plus the two PHerc0814 retention regions,25tiles each:800single-tile forwards. Reuse baseline maps without new baseline inference. Same source arrays, layer starts, robust normalization, Hann blending, masks and forward convention. Unknown optical registration residual and prior model/teacher ancestry preclude independent ink-accuracy claims. No replacement of difficult regions, input recentering or mask shrinkage.

Primary measure: AP within every region, equal-context means per physical parent, then equal weight to Paris1/Paris2. PHerc0814 is a retention diagnostic, not a near-perfect improvement target. Success screen at each retained endpoint: Paris equal-parent AP gain≥.01 versus seed42, and no one of eight regions loses more than.02 AP or AUROC. These are practical development margins, not statistical significance. Budget contrasts are A512−A32/B512−B32; objective contrasts B32−A32/B512−A512; material contrast≥.01 AP.
