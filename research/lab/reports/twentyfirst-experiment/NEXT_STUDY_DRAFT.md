# Next research decision after preservation allocation

This is a post-result research recommendation, not an execution-ready protocol or authorization for paid resources. It supersedes older pending-study suggestions without changing their evidence.

## Decision

Retain seed42. Stop small objective/architecture sweeps against the same six Paris development contexts. The allocation experiment answered its narrow question: preservation improved on the selected PHerc0139 support, but the required development gains did not follow. Repeating those regions does not create independent evidence, and extra GPU capacity would not resolve their provenance or repeated-use limitations.

## Next useful work

1. **Build an evaluation contract with defensible parent and teacher separation.** Inventory candidate parents, scans, aliases, registered optical truth, labeling policy and upstream teacher exposure using metadata and sources first. Reserve the final subjects before inspecting model outputs. Report unresolved ancestry directly; a different crop of an exposed parent is not automatically an independent holdout. Frag6 remains reserved, but its missing render/source contract prevents treating the completed sparse geometry audit as native alignment or labeled model validation.
2. **Finish source-compatible input validation before comparing a native checkpoint.** Its inspected safetensors schema matches the current factory's expected names/shapes and alias groups. A future bounded full-file check must verify the advertised digest, finite tensor values and equality within expected alias groups before canonicalization or strict loading. Then separately verify the pinned native model's depth, scale, normalization, architecture construction and inference recipe. Freeze a zero-training parity test before comparing accuracy. No silent key dropping, guessed plane selection or change to the present baseline is justified by header compatibility.
3. **Use geometry to define honest computational support.** The public PHerc0139 mesh work can establish dependencies and coordinate bounds under a declared arithmetic path. It cannot reconstruct missing historical private masks or observed native tissue coverage by itself. Any future real-volume reproduction needs exact source identifiers and interpolation/downsampling rules, a fixed sparse query budget and comparison tolerances set before pixels are read. Source-independent boundary and sentinel tests remain useful CPU work.

These tasks should produce a single new, falsifiable study design. Prefer a design that separates a geometry/input correction from any change in supervision, model capacity or preservation objective. Keep original initialization, matched controls and common diagnostics where they answer that question. Do not choose the next intervention or decision threshold by repeatedly optimizing Paris2 development scores.
