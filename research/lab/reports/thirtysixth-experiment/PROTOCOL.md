# Fixed CT texture-transition prototype

This is a post-inspection development study on four cached Frag6/PHerc51 CT stacks. The purpose is a possible structural depth reference for Plinius, not ink detection or an authenticated material surface. No optical image, ink label, model score or reserve enters the computation. No model selection, source acquisition or GPU work is authorized by this protocol.

Inputs are phase35's independently verified 63×128×128 float64 spatial-mean CT arrays. Central x/y differences at one coarse pixel spacing are squared and summed, then averaged over a 7×7 coarse window. Discard a four-pixel spatial halo, leaving 120×120 outputs. Coordinates remain historical rendered layers. No physical voxel scale or anatomical orientation is inferred.

For boundaries between layers k and k+1, k=5..58, compare mean texture energy in the five immediately preceding and five following planes. Divide the signed difference by the maximum five-plane mean energy anywhere on that ray; a zero scale produces zero contrast. Select greatest absolute normalized contrast, breaking ties at the first candidate. Accept only contrast ≥0.5 and no candidate more than five layers away whose magnitude is ≥0.8 of the maximum. Otherwise abstain. Accepted depth is k+0.5, and sign indicates rising/falling texture only. These fixed thresholds are engineering choices, not calibrated probabilities. Narrow transitions within five layers can merge; broad, weak or multiple changes can be rejected. A noise change can mimic material structure. Preserve diagnostic maps even on abstention.

Before real-map evaluation run seven mechanics fixtures and 41 prespecified synthetic cases: seeds0..7 for single, sloped, double, noise-only and gradual-ramp texture, plus one exact flat case. Single and sloped cases must each have ≥95% accepted coverage and ≥95% of all valid pixels accepted and within1 and2 layers of known truth, respectively. Each negative case must accept ≤5%. Every case must pass; aggregate success cannot hide a failed seed. If this gate fails, stop real evaluation and preserve failure. Do not tune the fixed rule on these cases to manufacture a pass.

Only after synthetic success, evaluate all four cached real contexts once. Report acceptance, signed orientation, full boundary distributions, normalized contrast and competing-transition ratio. A three-plane moving mean along depth (nearest endpoint padding) is a predeclared robustness perturbation, reported alongside baseline; no variant is selected as a winner. Report agreement among jointly accepted pixels and loss/gain of acceptance. Positive affine intensity transform (1.25X+1000) is a mechanical invariance check, not a scanner-generalization test. Do not use labels or model metrics to judge either depth map. No anatomical or ink-depth ground truth exists here.

Freeze code, input hashes and this protocol before running the synthetic study or real maps. Retain failures. Limits: no network; each worker ≤120seconds and512MiB peak RSS; aggregate new phase output ≤64MiB. An independent offline arithmetic check may reconstruct all energies once and scalar boundaries at16 fixed sites per context, then inspect maps. This is implementation verification, not external ground truth. The same previously scored parent and inspected image data do not count as a new test set.
