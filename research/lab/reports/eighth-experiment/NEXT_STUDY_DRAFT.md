# Next-study decision after the negative continuation pilot

Post-result design notes, not a preregistered or executed experiment. The lead performed this scientific review directly, following the user's request. The eighth plan, outputs and failed gates remain immutable.

The immediate problem is preserving the strong initializer while learning from a small adaptation set. All three 32-step continuations reduced fresh-reference ranking and sensitivity relative to frozen seed42. C additionally raised the negative-score cutoff needed on one calibration region, producing a large sensitivity penalty. Ordinary pseudo targets protected some sensitivity relative to manual-only continuation but still did not beat the frozen model.

The most informative next control is to separate the **shared change from smoothed targets to hard targets** from the act of continuing on a narrow region set. Retain the unchanged initializer and the completed unsmoothed arms as historical observations. A new matched experiment should explicitly include the original 0.5 BCE smoothing convention, with fixed data, optimizer, steps and calibration. It should avoid coupling that control to a new depth window, teacher, architecture or sampling distribution.

If preserving the original target convention is insufficient, evaluate initialization-preserving distillation or independently defined replay as a separate factor, with equal image/manual-label exposure and a frozen source for replay targets. Such regularization is established prior art; it is an engineering control, not a first-in-class claim. Do not combine smoothing restoration, replay, learning-rate changes and region balancing into one arm and attribute any gain to a single component. A later factorial comparison and multiple seeds should follow a promising small pilot.

Before freezing that study, audit how representative the retention references are. Frozen seed42's near-perfect agreement on these supplied-supervision crops occurs in a known historical model-development corpus with incomplete annotation/teacher lineage. It cannot serve as an external-generalization benchmark. Current fresh regions are now development evidence. Reserve new physical support before another adaptive comparison, and work toward references with stronger human/optical provenance and documented model/teacher exposure. A native/fine causal comparison still requires measured registration; the existing w035 lead does not supply it.

Keep the unchanged seed42 baseline and preserve A/B/C as negative-result checkpoints. No additional training, acquisition, checkpoint search or paid launch is authorized by this draft alone.
