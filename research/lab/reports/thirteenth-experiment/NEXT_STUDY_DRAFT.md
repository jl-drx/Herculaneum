# Post-result direction: calibration and stronger validation

This draft follows the completed Plinius conservative-continuation control. It is not an executed or frozen fourteenth experiment. Root owns scientific review; ordinary bounded CPU research remains authorized.

Both 1e-6 controls now improve fixed adaptation risk and pass the frozen ranking-retention screen. This removes the demonstrated immediate-overshoot objection to using this control. It does not establish an optimal rate, a novel adaptation technique or a deployable ink detector. At the same original cutoff, sensitivity decreases in both reference domains; Frag4 was weak at that cutoff even for the baseline.

The next discriminating test should change **calibration only**, leaving all three candidate/baseline weight identities, input processing and diagnostic support unchanged. Reuse the existing designated development regions and pinned negative-only threshold-selection procedure after verifying its exact contract. Write each threshold before applying it to the retained diagnostic maps. Frag4, its aliases, and the diagnostic PHerc0814 regions must not supply fitted thresholds, preprocessing choices or model selection. Do not search thresholds on their ROC/PR curves and relabel the result as transfer.

Predeclare primary operating criteria, per-context results and comparisons to both the common-cutoff results and frozen baseline before running the new development inference. The question is whether development-only calibration restores useful sensitivity at the required specificity on the unchanged diagnostic regions. Ranking itself must remain identical when only the cutoff changes. A failure is informative about threshold transfer across domains, not a reason to refit on the diagnostic labels. A success still does not make these reused regions a new holdout.

There is no need to train another model, add a loss coefficient, choose a best checkpoint or launch another learning-rate sweep for this question. A and B should remain matched, credited adaptations of the published seed42 architecture. The working name Plinius identifies the research series, not demonstrated novelty. Do not promote B merely because its Frag4 mean is slightly better here.

In parallel with later method development, improve the validation contract: obtain genuinely reserved physical support with defensible annotation/depth mapping and traceable exposure history. The modern PHerc0009B mapping/depth provenance gap remains unresolved; do not invent it to expand a benchmark. Frag4's manual optical-derived labels still have unmeasured registration residual and incompletely verified historical exposure. Multiple crops from one specimen cannot replace independent specimens or authenticated reading.

Only after stable optimization and a defensible operating/validation protocol should a new methodological contribution be tested against these controls. Preservation KL, small learning rates and threshold recalibration are established techniques. A frontier claim needs a defined new mechanism, matched controls and evidence beyond this small retrospective benchmark.

The calibration control remains economical on CPU. A later GPU pilot still needs a useful frozen workload, scoped worker-secret delivery, pinned CUDA environment, current all-in pricing and independent shutdown. Preserve the £10 TOTAL cap, including failures/retries and attributable overhead. Current GPU spend is £0; no paid resource is required by this draft. No publication, upload, outreach or prize submission follows automatically from a screen pass.
