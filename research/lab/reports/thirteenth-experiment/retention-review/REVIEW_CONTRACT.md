This is an independent source and saved-result review of the conditional thirteenth retention comparison. It is not scientific execution approval. No actual model, checkpoint or source array is read during preparation. Root owns training, inference and scientific decisions; earlier frozen artifacts remain unchanged.

The retention trigger must use each new arm's final fixed-schedule adaptation risk, compared with its own identical initializer risk. Both base and full risk changes must be at most −1e-4 after the fixed 32 updates. Immediate same-batch losses, historical online preupdate losses and retained evaluation scores cannot substitute for this gate. Qualifying arms are a subset of A_preserve and B_preserve; no replacement, orientation choice, new checkpoint choice or retry is permitted. With no qualifying arm, the comparison must explicitly skip new inference.

The fixed panel consists of the two previously used PHerc0814 retention contexts and the two previously used Frag4 contexts. Each PHerc0814 input retains its real 21-plane uint8 surface with model planes [2,19), full unmasked patch normalization and the fixed 64-pixel primary margin. Each Frag4 input retains its real 17-plane float32 surface with model planes [0,17), the same full-patch normalizer and fixed 32-pixel primary margin. All new runs use forward depth order. Inputs, support masks and supplied labels must be exactly those pinned in the previous studies. A fixed primary pixel cannot silently disappear because a score is nonfinite or unevaluated.

At most two newly trained states run four contexts each: eight runs and 200 tile forwards in total. Baseline and matched old 1e-4 continuation scores are reused from their original pinned files. There are no baseline forwards, new source acquisitions, calibration calls or cutoff fitting. If the protocol uses the retained baseline primary cutoff for every role, that common rule must be frozen and described explicitly; it is different from the earlier reports' own-role operating cutoffs, even though no cutoff is refitted.

The independent scorer will use the already tested grouped positive/negative concordance implementation for tied-score AUC, grouped suffix counts for average precision and float64(score) ≥ fixed cutoff for exact confusion counts. It will verify plan/result/source/selection/mask identities; complete eligible-arm coverage; all fixed participating pixels evaluated and finite; captured raw-logit shapes and tile placements; common normalized-input fingerprints across new arms where recorded; and equality of the evaluation domain across reused and new maps. Semantic state hashes are checked against frozen source and worker receipts, without constructing or deserializing a model.

The checker will recompute every reported per-context metric, baseline-relative and matched-old deltas, equal-context summaries and separately declared equal-specimen summaries. It will retain fixed role names and complete reporting. No retention statistic may feed back into the trigger or select a learning rate, arm, threshold, depth direction or context.

The frozen descriptive retention screen compares each specimen/domain's mean AUC and AP with baseline, allowing a drop of at most 0.01, and each context's AUC and AP with baseline, allowing a drop of at most 0.02. All comparisons are inclusive, use absolute metric differences and have no gate tolerance. The common threshold is the retained seed42 primary cutoff 0.7558947205543518; its sensitivity and specificity are secondary metrics. Passing does not automatically promote a model. The fixed-risk training gate uses exact final-minus-initial equal-batch means, inclusive ≤ −1e-4, without a rounding tolerance.

The four contexts are two already seen specimens. Adjacent model tiles overlap, and contexts within a specimen are not independent specimen replicates. Training-risk qualification is selection on adaptation evidence. These outputs can describe prediction retention on reused supplied references; they cannot establish prospective accuracy, independence from historical model exposure, papyrological authenticity, novelty, significance or text. Raw-score numeric reconstruction is separate from worker-reported parameter, optimizer and semantic-state checks.
