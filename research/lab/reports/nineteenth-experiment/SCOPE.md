Three bounded source audits investigate PHerc0009B, PHerc0500P2 and optical Frag6 / PHerc51Cr4Fr8. No candidate is selected using model scores or decoded labels. The audits may read existing evidence and fetch public text/metadata only. Scroll and optical audits each have at most 24 requests / 16 MiB response-body allowance; upstream exposure audit has at most 20 requests / 12 MiB. Record successful and failed requests with source identities. No automatic retry loops, images, scientific arrays or checkpoint downloads. Retained historical pins and newly observed mutable pages must remain distinguishable.

Eligibility requires separate evidence for parent/alias identity, input geometry, label/support semantics, annotation and teacher lineage, applicable use restrictions, and training exposure. Absence from a released configuration does not prove absence from pretraining or teacher ancestry. Local freshness and upstream independence are separate claims. Neither uncertainty nor a successful download promotes a candidate to a clean confirmation set. All already inspected Paris contexts remain development only and excluded from gradients, teachers and cutoff fitting across aliases.

The prototype compares exactly matched raw-neighbor and explicit-depth feature branches at the existing 32-channel depth-fusion output, before the frozen 2D U-Net. It must preserve the original fused features and final output at zero initialization, propagate input gradients through the frozen decoder, and keep every released parameter and buffer unchanged. The branch remains a jointly changed feature bundle; this phase cannot separate coordinate and derivative effects or attribute differences against the prior 801-parameter experiment solely to insertion point.

Initial implementation checks use synthetic feature tensors and a toy frozen decoder only. A subsequent actual-checkpoint integration check may be executed by the root after code review and a pinned plan, with deterministic synthetic inputs only. This is an engineering test, not an ink-detection training experiment: no scientific pixels, real labels, held-out acquisition, performance scoring or cutoff fitting. Use the retained strict seed42 loader without vendor edits. Maximum 300 seconds, four CPU threads, 4 GiB RSS; no GPU, dependency installation or network during model execution. Preserve failures and do not loosen checks to make them pass.
