# Phase 19 upstream exposure audit

None of the three proposed parents is present in seed42's **29 declared training representations**, but this does not establish a fully independent upstream holdout. The stronger new distinction is model-specific: the published paper's ResNet3D-50 baseline explicitly reports training on PHerc0009B and PHerc0500P2 aliases, while the production canonical ResNet-152 teacher is a different model whose released configuration does not identify training specimens. We found no artifact proving that either exact model generated seed42's original annotations. Unknown lineage is not proven contamination.

Seed42's embedded provenance explicitly records `backbone_pretrained_path: null` and `backbone_pretrained_sha256: null`. Its model settings name the local 3D stem and ordinary 2D U-Net, without a configured pretrained backbone. The accurate wording is **“no configured pretrained backbone; original annotation and teacher ancestry unresolved.”** The supplied configuration and reconstruction source describe the released recipe; they are not a complete historical training log.

This audit used eight bounded public text/metadata requests and retained text only. It fetched no checkpoint, CT, annotation image, model prediction, array chunk or other scientific payload. No model was loaded or executed, and no existing evidence was changed. `request-ledger.json` records each request's URL, UTC times, status, body length and SHA-256; `retained-source-pins.json` binds the 17 retained inputs read here. URLs described as mutable snapshots must not be treated as versions of the historical training bytes.

## Operational rule for the next study

1. **Separate the claims.** Track direct model training exposure, weight initialization, original annotation/teacher ancestry, local adaptation/development exposure and label independence as separate fields. Do not collapse them into a single “clean” flag.
2. **Local adaptation holdout is achievable without pretending to know all upstream history.** Freeze a candidate's whole parent and known aliases out of gradients, preservation-teacher inputs, pseudo-label construction, cutoff fitting and architecture selection. Freeze acquisition, geometry, support, endpoints and reporting before model outputs. Report performance as a prospective local transfer/reference-agreement test with the unresolved upstream fields disclosed. Once inspected results influence design, retire it to development status.
3. **Stronger unseen-parent claims require a stronger chain.** For seed42 or a candidate inheriting it, obtain versioned training/support manifests and label-production lineage sufficient to exclude the parent from direct and indirect exposure. For the paper baseline, PHerc0009B and PHerc0500P2 already fail parent exclusion. For production R152, exposure remains unknown; do not assign the paper model's history to it.
4. **Independent ink accuracy also requires independent labels.** A prediction-aligned or iterative pseudo-label reference can measure agreement but does not become optical truth through train/test exclusion. Human annotations, optical registration and their use of model assistance must be authenticated separately.
5. **Do not spend a GPU budget to resolve provenance.** The next useful work is to complete the selected source/geometry/annotation contract, then freeze a bounded comparison. These findings support continued development research now; they do not certify a clean confirmatory set or imply a paid launch.

No outreach, credentials, configuration save, publication or paid action was performed by this audit. The total first-GPU-pilot cap remains £10; spend in this audit is £0.
