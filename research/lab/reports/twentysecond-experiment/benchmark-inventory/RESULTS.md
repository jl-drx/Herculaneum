## Evidence that changes the interpretation

The paper R3D50 and production canonical R152 are distinct artifacts. The [paper model card](/workspace/herculaneum-lab/data/native-reference-preflight-20261008/iteration-0-README.md:19), SHA256 `b7f406cdc51a7874ca3f786961f744286221c890f307e043255c41577e962a78`, explicitly names `500p2a`, `20250910185200` and the exact PHerc0009B auto-grown candidate. Its [pipeline metadata](/workspace/herculaneum-lab/data/native-reference-preflight-20261008/paper-ink-detection__metadata.json:50), SHA256 `6cfaf7c64cd286da9e9f302df663e86d36573ec17b25a89dd64ff3c2644deaf8`, supplies parent bridges and both train/validation segment lists (lines 188–204). Shared segment membership does not demonstrate shared loss pixels. The separate [canonical R152 card](/workspace/herculaneum-lab/reports/nineteenth-experiment/exposure-audit/canonical-readme.body), SHA256 `a9eaef931e8ada20f484700ba355ccc0f926126133623745ca883b8452badc0a`, and five-field config do not disclose its training set. No retained artifact proves the paper R3D50 created seed42's original labels; the inventory does not invent that ancestry edge.

Seed42 records no configured pretrained backbone (`backbone_pretrained_path` and SHA both null), but original annotation/teacher ancestry remains unresolved. The [phase19 artifact-specific audit](/workspace/herculaneum-lab/reports/nineteenth-experiment/exposure-audit/AUDIT.md:5) makes that distinction. Nieuwlaar's published configuration retains the four original parent families and adds ten PHerc0139 native segments, initialized from Russo ex016. Its [teacher table](/workspace/herculaneum-lab/reports/frontier-methods-audit-20261008/source-evidence/training/25d17fdb764e92fbea419b804bd1f0a831ed4555271b4bc8bccdd0aeab9acace-nieuwlaar-hf-training__teacher_sources.tsv:1) identifies canonical 78-keV maps, not their teacher's complete training history. Direct model roots and teacher-map application are different evidence channels.

PHerc0841's exact primary source is the retained official catalog, compressed SHA256 `7ec8dcaa3000b9716724c26a1991ee416491d8a6b3615238ca90ae11540abdd3`. JSON pointer `/samples/PHerc0841/segments/20260221022814/data/11/creation_info/quality/validity_decision/note` says “the letters were aggressively completed, iteration by iteration” and “much of the annotation is reconstructed letter form.” The literal [extract](/workspace/herculaneum-lab/reports/twentysecond-experiment/benchmark-inventory/CATALOG_EXTRACTS.json:1443) retains it. The w00 record separately reports 763,690 labeled pixels outside supervision (12.3881%); its sibling `20260220214732` declined a prediction-driven shift because the gain was below the publisher's cutoff. These are publisher metadata claims, not measurements reproduced here. All three are one parent; selecting the sibling with the best reported model agreement would introduce another selection channel.

All five modern candidate segments have catalog-listed predictions from model `20260417190342/new_canon_autoresearch_recipe`. That proves deployed inference metadata, not training. Their quality notes call the reviewing prediction “independent,” but do not identify an artifact hash or an exposure manifest; co-location does not prove that the listed map is the exact review input. The generic specimen photographs in the catalog do not authenticate optical ink labels or confidently negative support.

The [phase19 package audit](/workspace/herculaneum-lab/reports/nineteenth-experiment/scroll-audit/RESULTS.md:17) already established complete 39-object inventories for PHerc0009B and PHerc0500P2, with no mapping-validity or linked provenance assets. Its four exact 404s and dirty-publisher/2D-versus-65-depth source discrepancy remain unresolved. Metadata inventory alone cannot repair them. It also resolved mesh-relative centers to 54 and 58.5, respectively; neither establishes annotation depth or authorizes the old pooled window. Those resolved questions should not be re-fetched without evidence of a changed release.

Re-reading an unchanged model card, package listing or generic rendering implementation cannot settle those historical facts.
