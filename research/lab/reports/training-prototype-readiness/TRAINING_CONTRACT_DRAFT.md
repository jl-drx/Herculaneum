# Three-arm training prototype — draft, not ready to train

The seventh panel found ex016 AUC gains in3/4 contexts and AP gains in4/4, alongside weaker threshold transfer. This overturns a broad extrapolation from the sixth crop's negative result. It motivates a controlled supervision/operating-point hypothesis, not adoption by prize rank, an authentic-ink accuracy claim or a new-method claim. The [research charter](../../RESEARCH_CHARTER.md) governs this work.

Provisional common initialization is the retained strict seed42 checkpoint, chosen for continuity and verified technical compatibility, not because it won the new panel. Its historical training exposure remains unresolved. Reinitialize every arm from the exact same bytes; do not initialize different arms from control/ex016 or a newly selected winning model. Teacher identity and frozen pseudo-label bytes are separate from student initialization.

The proposed arms are manual-only continuation, ordinary hard-pseudo continuation, and the same dense pseudo supervision with manual positive/negative anchors balanced by region and class. Keep initialization, input/batch order, augmentation draws, optimizer/schedule, steps, total image exposure and sampled manual pixels identical. Every batch must retain manual support so a pseudo-only skipped update cannot silently change the manual-only arm's schedule. Counts and loss weights are distinct: equal manual exposure does not imply equal gradients.

A useful explicit loss contract is A: uniform manual masked BCE; B: the same manual term plus a fixed normalized pseudo term; C: a fixed convex redistribution of that manual term between uniform BCE and equal-region/equal-class manual-anchor BCE, plus exactly B's pseudo term. This preserves total manual coefficient while changing anchor distribution. Pseudo and anchor coefficients, conflict rules, source-pixel normalization and minimum class/region support are **null/unfrozen**. Do not infer teacher-background labels from unknown pixels. Supplied manual/public annotations require lineage review and are not automatically independent truth. These three arms will not isolate every mechanism; coverage/manual-weight/teacher-quality factorial controls remain necessary later.

Before real training, freeze data/teacher/label versions and physical support; accepted anchor uncertainty and positive/negative participation masks; split assignments with development separated from reserved physical groups; common initialization; batch/augmentation seed ledger; optimizer/schedule/step count; loss/normalization coefficients; output/checkpoint policy; metrics and stop rules. Current three-scroll annotations have prior training/online-validation and teacher uncertainty. They cannot be relabeled a globally held-out test. Native versus pooled input comparisons require verified physical registration and separate source contracts. No actual training data, new teacher targets or final split is selected here.

The accompanying synthetic checker performs one plain masked BCE SGD step only on a fixed-seed artificial[1,1,17,128,128] input. It uses strict unchanged seed42/factory, requires known positive and negative pixels, leaves outside participation unknown with exactly zero logits gradients, checks finite gradients and actual parameter change, and reports time/RSS. It separately computes an illustrative equal-region/equal-class anchor loss without stepping it. This verifies mechanics only; neither SGD settings nor this toy anchor loss are validated research choices. No images, predictions or updated weights are saved; updated weights are discarded.

Root alone executes the hashed synthetic plan/script. Limits are60seconds wall,120CPU seconds, four threads,8GiB hard address space and3GiB RSS acceptance, offline, no dependencies or real data. A passing toy does not establish GPU fit or usable training throughput. Real training/GPU readiness remains false. Any paid pilot requires the separately approved frozen CUDA workload, current quote, scoped worker delivery and independent shutdown, within the existing £10 aggregate cap including failures/overhead. No paid launch, new acquisition, publication or R2 write is authorized by this draft.
