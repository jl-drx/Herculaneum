# Static support and objective review

This review reads retained Python source, plans and JSON receipts only. It does not load arrays, import execution helpers, construct models, load checkpoints, access the network or independently validate numerical exposure counts. The ninth and tenth workers inherit the eighth mask and loss helpers through hash-bound imports: [ninth control_common.py](../../ninth-experiment/implementation/control_common.py), lines 8–14, and [tenth control_common.py](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/control_common.py), lines 9–17.

Let `S` be supplied supervision participation, `V` public validation participation, and `O` the acquisition-observed mask. The actual manual support is `M = S & ~V & O`. Manual targets are binary supplied labels read only on `M`; other entries receive an unused `-1` placeholder. The actual pseudo support is `P = O & E43 & finite(score43) & ~M`, where `E43` is the retained seed 43 evaluated mask. Its hard target is `1[score43 >= 0.5]`. Retired `V` is openly eligible for `P`; it is excluded from manual labels, not from adaptation altogether. `M` and `P` are disjoint. Sources: [experiment_common.py](../../eighth-experiment/implementation/experiment_common.py), lines 75–88; initializer/teacher roles are checked at line 54.

`O` denotes acquisition support, not nonzero attenuation, ink or independent geometric validity. The loader requires `O` to be entirely true and separately verifies a nonzero-occupancy mask from selected layers `2:19`; that occupancy mask is **not** an additional factor in `M` or `P`. A pixel may therefore participate even when its own selected-depth values are all zero, provided acquisition and the relevant teacher-map support permit it. Sources: [experiment_common.py](../../eighth-experiment/implementation/experiment_common.py), lines 62–71 and 79–87.

The ninth control smooths participating hard targets to `0.25 + 0.5*y`. `U` and `P_loss` are separate BCE means over all participating pixels in the combined batch of two patches. They are not averages of two separately normalized regional means, and the pseudo term is not normalized jointly with manual pixels. The objectives are `A_smooth = U` and `B_smooth = U + 0.25*P_loss`. Thus regional and class influence within each uniform term depends on pixel counts. The computed diagnostic `R` instead averages four manual means equally: two regions × two hard classes. Each patch must contain at least 256 manual pixels of each class, but this anchor gate does not rebalance `U`. `R` is logged and **not optimized** by A/B; the retained `balanced_manual_mixture = 0.5` field belongs to the inherited C definition, which these controls do not execute. Sources: [training_mechanics.py](../../eighth-experiment/implementation/training_mechanics.py), lines 26–51; [smoothing.py](../../ninth-experiment/implementation/smoothing.py), lines 5–18; [ninth run_worker.py](../../ninth-experiment/implementation/run_worker.py), lines 43–60 and 87–90. The tenth worker retains the same batch/support construction at lines 62–89 of [run_worker.py](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/run_worker.py).

The tenth preservation teacher is a **different teacher**: an unchanged, detached seed 42 copy of the initializer. For the current normalized and augmented input patch, `q = sigmoid(teacher42_logits)` is a soft prediction, not the seed 43 hard pseudo target or a precomputed full-context seed 42 map. Teacher and student receive the identical tensor. Preservation uses temperature-one Bernoulli `KL(q || sigmoid(z))`, computed in float64, with separate masked means `D_M` and `D_P`. The implemented objectives are `A_preserve = U + D_M` and `B_preserve = U + 0.25*P_loss + D_M + 0.25*D_P`; overall preservation coefficient is 1. Sources: [preservation.py](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/preservation.py), lines 4–19; [tenth run_worker.py](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/run_worker.py), lines 74–94 and 182–189; [prepare_plan.py](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/prepare_plan.py), lines 25–30.

Pixels outside `M` in A and outside `M | P` in B have no direct output-loss participation. Both workers assert zero gradient at those output logits. This does not remove their input context: normalization uses the complete selected-depth patch, and convolutions can propagate input information into participating outputs. The assertion therefore does not establish zero input influence or an unchanged prediction at unknown locations. Sources: [ninth run_worker.py](../../ninth-experiment/implementation/run_worker.py), lines 45–48 and 77–79; [tenth run_worker.py](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/run_worker.py), lines 64–67 and 115–117.

After the root freezes and authorizes the numerical audit, retained masks, hard targets, schedule and per-step JSON exposure records can reconstruct manual/pseudo class mixtures, region shares, unique support, repeated exposure multiplicities and each term's denominators. Those are different quantities; repeated overlapping patches are not additional independent observations. This static review does not assert reconstructed counts. Sources: [training_mechanics.py](../../eighth-experiment/implementation/training_mechanics.py), lines 5–23; [ninth run_worker.py](../../ninth-experiment/implementation/run_worker.py), lines 50–56 and 87–92; [tenth run_worker.py](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/run_worker.py), lines 69–75 and 128–136. The final plans retain `schedule`, `exposure` and inherited training fields in [ninth control-plan.json](../../ninth-experiment/implementation/final-plan/control-plan.json), lines 1076, 1366 and 1398, and [tenth control-plan.json](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/final-plan/control-plan.json), lines 1076, 1420 and 1448.

Tenth student/teacher logits and losses are recorded **before each optimizer update**. Successive records use scheduled patches and augmentations, so they do not form a fixed-domain final-model loss trajectory. They cannot establish within-step post-update improvement, monotonic improvement or the final checkpoint's loss on the recorded inputs. The worker saves final weights, not intermediate weights or optimizer state. Exact first-step teacher/student agreement is an initialization check, not evidence that later preservation gradients are zero. Sources: [tenth run_worker.py](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/run_worker.py), lines 76–108, 125–136 and 155–164; [prepare_plan.py](../../tenth-experiment/implementation/prepare_plan.py), line 30. These are objective/support facts; scientific strategy and interpretation remain with the root review.
