This is a proposed fixed input-domain diagnostic, not an execution receipt. No arrays, model outputs or checkpoints have been loaded, and no array statistics have been computed for this phase. Root approval and a frozen executable package are required before the diagnostic runs.

Use exactly seven retained contexts: Frag4 context 1 and context 2; PHerc0139/w016 rank 01 and rank 02 used in adaptation; and the three existing calibration contexts, PHerc1667/w029 and PHerc0814/46527 rank 01 and rank 07. These are bound by the eleventh pooled-reference descriptor and tenth control plan. The two later PHerc0814 retention contexts are outside this first diagnostic. No regions are added or selected by predictions.

The documented changes are substantial but are not yet measured explanations of performance. Frag4 uses a legacy published surface volume at 54 keV and nominal 3.24 µm, with uint16 source TIFF planes. The new recipe directly sums 3×3×3 supplied samples in uint32, divides by 27×257 in float64 and casts once to float32, without integer quantization. Its 17 source-group centers are 8, 11, …, 56, with center 32 and nominal 9.72 µm spacing. Earlier contexts use 78 keV, nominal 2.399 µm published surface volumes at level 2, supplying uint8 XY planes at 9.596 µm. Consecutive depth groups of four are averaged in float32, rounded to nearest even and cast to uint8. Model input selects pooled planes [2,19), corresponding to source planes [21,89), with group-center indices 22.5, 26.5, …, 86.5 and center 54.5. The model remains a 17-plane model. Source-index centers are specimen-local conventions, not a measured cross-specimen registration.

Nominal sample spacing is 1.2922% larger for Frag4. The first-to-last center separation is 155.52 µm versus 153.536 µm. These metadata facts do not establish label-depth accuracy, scan transfer causality or an intensity calibration between acquisitions. The legacy annotation/support construction also differs from the modern transferred reference packages. All-source acquisition support, exposed-surface support and supplied annotation participation remain separately named.

Both recipes pass full 17×128×128 patches through the same retained robust-MAD normalizer. It copies to float32, computes preclip standard deviation, clips at the full-patch 1st and 99th percentiles, then subtracts the clipped median and divides by 1.4826 times the clipped MAD. When that scale is nonfinite or less than 1e-6, fallback preference is preclip standard deviation, then half the percentile span, then 1. Mask and annotation membership do not enter normalization. Entirely zero patches are skipped by the unchanged inference adapter. Thus a positive scalar conversion such as division by 257 ordinarily cancels under median/MAD normalization; quantization, percentile ties, precision, zero padding, degenerate-scale fallbacks and altered texture can still matter. Their actual frequency must be measured before an explanation is offered.

After approval, the diagnostic will compute the following fixed quantities without reading labels, prediction maps, logits or model weights:

- Raw selected-17 statistics in the whole context, fixed 64-pixel XY interior and separately named published-support masks: finite count, zero count/fraction, fractional-value count, minimum, 1/5/50/95/99 percentiles, maximum, mean, standard deviation, median and MAD. Nonzero occupancy is descriptive, never an automatic validity exclusion.
- Depth profiles at all 17 recorded group centers: the same raw summaries, adjacent-plane mean absolute difference and Pearson correlation. Constant-profile correlation is null. No depth shift, reversal search or fitted registration is performed.
- Spatial texture at fixed one-pixel X and Y lags: adjacent-sample mean absolute difference and Pearson correlation, with exact pair counts. Report intensity differences both per pixel and divided by the declared nominal spacing; those units are arbitrary source intensities per nominal µm, not calibrated attenuation.
- Full-patch normalizer diagnostics for every fixed stride-64 tile: raw zero fraction, preclip standard deviation, exact clip limits, fractions strictly below/above those limits and tied at each limit, clipped median/MAD, selected denominator and fallback reason, plus normalized range, percentiles, mean, standard deviation and zero fraction. Compare the traced result with the actual pinned NumPy normalizer. Preserve skipped all-zero tiles explicitly. The grid has 159 tiles in total: 50 Frag4, 50 adaptation and 59 calibration.
- A separate adaptation subset summary for the 12 unique tiles named by the already frozen 32-step schedule. Count repeated scheduled exposure separately from unique tile summaries. XY D4 changes sample placement but preserves the patch-value multiset and these scalar normalizer statistics; no augmentation or model forward is needed.

The main comparison domain is the entire retained input, exactly as seen by normalization. A common fixed 64-pixel interior is a descriptive secondary domain, not a replacement for the historical Frag4 32-pixel evaluation margin. For old contexts, the acquired-observed mask means encoded source presence and does not authenticate physical surface geometry. For Frag4, the supplied surface mask identifies published exposed-surface support; it is not an acquisition-absence mask. Annotation masks are counted separately for context only: Frag4 known/primary; old supervision/validation. No ink-label-conditioned intensity statistic is proposed.

The output will be a small JSON receipt, per-context/per-tile summaries and a concise input audit. Descriptions may compare fixed contexts and tabulate observed differences, but will not fit a domain correction, calibrate an offset or threshold, associate statistics with selected score peaks, compute causal attribution or assert measured optical registration. No new preprocessing, model calls or scientific rerun is authorized by this proposal. Proposed execution limits are 120 seconds wall time, 512 MiB RSS, at most seven surface-array loads and 159 normalizer calls, with no network. All bound input files are rehashed before and after execution; failure leaves a STOPPED receipt.
