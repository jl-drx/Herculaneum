The approved diagnostic adds one fixed scale sanity check for each nonempty Frag4 tile: compare the unchanged normalizer applied to the retained float32 patch with the same normalizer applied to float32(257 × patch). Record exact element equality, maximum and mean absolute difference, the 99th percentile absolute difference and root mean square difference. There is no pass threshold, new input correction, model run or score association. This measures the scalar-conversion effect within the retained numeric representation; it cannot recreate lost source precision or authenticate registration.

The 159 fixed tiles are unchanged. The maximum number of actual normalizer calls is now 209: one per tile, plus one for each of the 50 Frag4 tiles. A separately traced NumPy calculation uses no additional call and must match the pinned normalizer exactly. Empty raw patches are retained as skipped, matching the inference adapter. The original proposed 159-call cap in input-audit-proposal.json is superseded only by this addition and the final frozen input-audit-plan.json. All other proposed scope and limits remain.

Tile overlap and physical dependence will be explicitly recorded. Adjacent stride-64 tiles overlap by 64 pixels in each axis. The two Frag4 contexts belong to one specimen, the two adaptation contexts to one PHerc0139 specimen, and the two PHerc0814 calibration contexts to one PHerc0814 specimen. Unique training tiles are a subset of the full grid, and repeated scheduled exposures do not create independent samples. Distributional similarity cannot rule out a semantic acquisition, optical-registration, annotation or teacher-lineage domain shift. Observed differences likewise do not identify their cause.
