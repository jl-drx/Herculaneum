# Phase47 portability

This study contains one fixed512×512 region and a bounded set of per-batch
probability arrays. Its replay bundle preserves the corrected four maps, original
failed baseline, all saved tiles/sums, canonical blend weights, frozen schedules,
receipts, valid mask, geometry screen and analysis/report files. No raw CT volume,
model weight, credential or installed environment is included.

The replay can independently reconstruct each saved ROI from its tile outputs
without model execution. Saved original scripts retain their lab paths; a
relocated replay wrapper provides verified replacements. The earlier PHerc343
workspace input/model bundles are required for fresh inference. Target geometry
source arrays are in the historical workspace snapshot, while compact target
screen outputs are included here. A checksum restore does not certify new-machine
inference behavior or the physical independence of a target field.

All source/code revisions and failed receipts remain part of the record. Earlier
phase46 files and their cumulative seal are unchanged. GPU spend remains£0.
