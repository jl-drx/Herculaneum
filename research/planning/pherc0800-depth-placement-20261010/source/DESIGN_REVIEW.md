# Assessment of the proposed next comparison

The bounded comparison is technically feasible and answers a narrower useful
question than "does PHerc0800 contain readable ink?" It changes the depth window
substantially while holding model identity, preprocessing, in-plane grids,
support, specimen and display fixed. It does not qualify a training method or
establish the correct papyrus surface.

## Is this a repetition?

Studies1 and3–5 used centred 17-plane Villa inputs, ±2-plane changes and reversal;
the shifts retained15/17 original planes. Studies49–51 qualified the 66-layer
render and the four-model recipe, then tested only d0. Study46 reproduced the
published PHerc343 display at its fixed d+1 setting; it did not execute the
author's full 31-centre sweep. The proposed PHerc0800 −15/0/+15 screen has not
been run in this retained record. It is distinct from the rejected trained
depth/fusion adapters and does not reopen the completed tiling-origin search.

The ±15 shifts retain only2/17 native Villa planes from the d0 window. Hecate
uses the same physical centre shifts with its original depth interpolation.
This makes a stronger input-placement perturbation than ±2, while avoiding an
unbounded centre sweep. It is deliberately coarse: an intermediate centre could
still matter, so a null result does not eliminate placement uncertainty.

## What genuinely blocks it?

No unresolved optical/CT alignment is needed for this **unlabelled response
comparison**: no optical label, supervised objective or AP is used. The retained
render's source correspondence and complete observed contexts were already
qualified, and new preflight checks preserve them. The separate problem of
which physical wrap or writing face was sampled remains unresolved. That limits
scientific interpretation and advancement, even though it does not block
computing the comparison. M7 agreement is not independent surface truth.

The execution blockers are concrete: the shifted worker still needs to preserve
original preprocessing and cohort arithmetic; its new depth-bound cache and
restart behavior must be qualified; and durable backup must be prepared. Input
feasibility alone must not silently count as passing those checks.

## Why this region and budget?

The entire middle row of the fixed study51 partition preserves its 1536-pixel
horizontal context, with almost complete inherited support. It avoids choosing
a detector hotspot or returning to a single128-square. All three cells are
previously reviewed development regions. Neither a different crop size nor the
unused depths makes the specimen a held-out benchmark.

All four models are retained to avoid selecting dense_native merely because its
padding correlation was higher. Both orders stay visible because the reading
side is not known. Both padding phases test whether a proposed structure stays
in the same source location. The reduced schedule retains complete original
cohorts, and every centre-depth baseline map now reconstructs exactly from saved
logits. This provides a direct test that the smaller scored domain did not
introduce a new grid artifact.

The 32 shifted maps require3,096 batches; measured study51 job times imply about
3h09m CPU, excluding new qualification and non-scaling overhead. This is an
affordable bounded research comparison under the user's unrestricted CPU budget,
not a powered accuracy experiment. No GPU criterion is inferred from it.

## What would change the decision?

Only a coherent candidate at a nonzero offset, present in both padding phases
and corroborated by the two model families, earns independent image/geometry
review. Reversal invariance is not required. Such corroboration is a conservative
lead-selection rule, not statistically independent validation; shared training
and material texture can still explain it. The rule can miss a real feature
visible to only one family, which must be retained as a weak lead rather than
discarded or promoted.

After the fixed comparison, stop if no candidate qualifies. Do not extend depth
or crop coverage in response to brightness alone. The recommended next research
route in that case is qualification of the PHerc0139 paired-acquisition
benchmark, subject to actual correspondence checks. This is a research direction,
not a claim that its pairs are already registered or its reference is ink truth.
