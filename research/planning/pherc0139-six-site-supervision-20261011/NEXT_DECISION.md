# Research consequence

This completes the bounded question; do not expand this local crop diagnostic
by searching for brighter patches or loosening the CT registration criteria.
Only two of 24 patches have the predeclared class balance, so this is useful
evidence about target sensitivity, not a representative estimate of paired
learning potential or evidence against ink recoverability.

Three decisions follow:

1. **Do not treat CT-derived transfers as precise hard truth.** The two
   informative patches both have material hard and soft sensitivity. Soft
   targets remain a possible learning choice, but were not shown to solve the
   registration problem. Site-level CT NCC is not a per-pixel ink-plane test.
2. **Do not promote uncertainty averaging as a distinct technique here.** Its
   matched stationary smoothing control reproduces it extremely closely. Keep
   that control in any future learned comparison; omit a separate averaging
   arm unless a new mechanism makes a distinguishable prediction.
3. **Use the ongoing native-learning study as the next decision point.** Finish
   the frozen nine fits and sealed evaluation. It tests native observation
   learnability with native-frame annotations without relying on this fine-to-
   coarse transfer. Training fit alone will not answer generalisation. Retain
   the ceiling-without-confirmed-plateau classification wherever it occurs;
   completion at the cap is not a convergence certificate.

Paired-acquisition learning remains a research hypothesis. The next learned
proposal should explicitly distinguish native annotation agreement, current
teacher agreement and physical ink. A teacher-target study would need adequate
region coverage, an admitted reference whose independence is understood, native
versus additional-supervision controls, and matched smoothing. The present
six sites do not establish those missing conditions. No paired training starts
from this sensitivity packet alone, and no more alignment-only sweeps are
recommended as a prerequisite by default.

GPU readiness is a separate implementation and cost question, not an arbitrary
requirement that tiny CPU runs first clear an AP threshold. For future heavy
work, use the measured runtime and a frozen informative comparison to assess
the £10 total pilot cap, current GPU rates, supported secret delivery and an
independent shutdown mechanism. This audit launches no paid resource and
does not restart, extend, stop or otherwise change the current CPU worker.
