# Independent scientific review of study52

11 October 2026. This review read the frozen protocol, prior completed studies and retained primary model documentation. It did **not** open any partial study52 predictions or response metrics, run inference, or alter the running study. Recommendations below concern interpretation and separately specified future work; the frozen gate remains unchanged.

**Assessment:** study52 is a useful, bounded test of whether substantial depth-window displacement reveals a reproducible target candidate. It is not a test with known sensitivity to ink, and a negative result cannot select among weak signal, acquisition transfer, surface error and an insensitive review criterion. The original PHerc343 reproduction qualified a different endpoint. A new read-only calibration performed during this review now shows that several strong reported structures do survive the *current individual-map family/padding display*; weaker cases remain ambiguous. See [the completed calibration](CALIBRATION_RESULTS.md). This supports the gate for strong-lead triage without establishing sensitivity to unknown target ink.

## What this experiment can identify

The held-fixed specimen, models, source support, grids and preprocessing make changes in response attributable to changing the sampled depth window within this particular recipe. The protocol keeps the whole middle row of the existing partition, not a response-selected hotspot ([protocol](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/in-progress/pherc0800-depth-20261011/source/PROTOCOL.md:18)). This is materially stronger than the old ±2-plane controls: the ±15 Villa shifts share only two of seventeen planes with the central input ([design review](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/planning/pherc0800-depth-placement-20261010/source/DESIGN_REVIEW.md:19)).

It does **not** isolate an ink-bearing surface. Moving along the normal changes tissue and potentially the wrap; the same flattened XY coordinate is a common coordinate convention, not proof of the same physical sheet. Sparse M7 profiles contained multiple predicted runs at 57 of 104 usable sites. On the actual segment, the nearest-run median was one voxel, seven of thirty measurable sites exceeded two voxels, and two tied ([geometry results](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/lab/reports/fortyfifth-experiment/GEOMETRY_RESULTS.md:7)). Those observations support local ambiguity, not a global ±15 correction or an independently measured placement error.

The screen also does not measure the model's depth capture range. Villa's three sets of integer input indices are 10–26, 25–41 and 40–56, whose union contains every index 10–56. Thus it is incorrect to say that the three windows leave most of that slab unread. It is equally incorrect to infer adequate *detection* coverage from that union: an ink trace appearing near a window edge need not be recognized as well as one near its learned preferred position. Centres fifteen voxels apart leave points as far as 7.5 voxels from the nearest centre between the endpoints. Whether this is tolerable is unmeasured. Hecate's different interpolation and half-voxel centre convention must remain distinct ([protocol](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/in-progress/pherc0800-depth-20261011/source/PROTOCOL.md:41)). Its primary documentation explicitly describes learned depth attention and dependence on sheet placement ([Hecate card](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/lab/reports/fortyfifth-experiment/source-evidence/hecate-README.md:28), [limitations](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/lab/reports/fortyfifth-experiment/source-evidence/hecate-README.md:90)).

Consequently, a change confined to one nonzero centre demonstrates **depth-dependent detector response**. Calling it recovered writing additionally requires the frozen visual corroboration and then independent physical/image review. No candidate, or high agreement across depths, has no unique mechanistic interpretation.

## The current gate is conservative, but its sensitivity is unknown

The frozen criterion requires a coherent new nonzero-depth structure at the same source location in both phases, corroborated by Villa and Hecate; reversal invariance is explicitly unnecessary ([protocol](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/in-progress/pherc0800-depth-20261011/source/PROTOCOL.md:104)). Preserve it.

Three limitations matter:

1. **Architectural corroboration is not independent evidence of truth.** Different families can respond to the same fibres, cracks, correlated teachers or neighboring wrap. Conversely, a genuine weak trace can fail because only one family detects it. The conjunction controls which leads consume follow-up effort; it has no measured false-positive rate or recall. Three Villa checkpoints do not constitute three independent specimen replications.
2. **The positive-control endpoint changed.** Study46 reproduced the published ownership-merged, equally z-scored four-model composite and inspected all eighteen exposed boxes in that display ([completion review](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/lab/reports/fortysixth-experiment/completion-review/README.md:52), [visual outcome](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/lab/reports/fortysixth-experiment/completion-review/README.md:80)). Study52 asks for corroboration in individual maps at fixed 0–255 ([protocol](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/in-progress/pherc0800-depth-20261011/source/PROTOCOL.md:95)). The earlier successful reproduction therefore does not establish sensitivity of the stricter present criterion. In study51, Hecate's darker background and Villa's diffuse responses already demonstrated differing map calibration ([results](/workspace/herculaneum-lab/reports/fiftyfirst-experiment/RESULTS.md:63)). No contrast fitting or retrospective gate change is justified, but the distinction must accompany a negative conclusion.
3. **The criterion is qualitative.** “Coherent,” “same location” and “corroboration” have no frozen numerical matching tolerance. This is acceptable for exploratory candidate triage, but it cannot support a significance claim from 48 views or millions of correlated pixels. The completion review should retain an explicit yes/no/uncertain judgment and rationale for each examined candidate, including contrary maps. Do not resolve uncertainty by trying new thresholds, depths or crops. This makes the existing judgment reviewable without inventing a new success threshold.

Low forward/reverse correlation is not a failing-control result: study51 already found approximately 0.077–0.166, and the two directions are neither known negatives nor required equivalent inputs ([results](/workspace/herculaneum-lab/reports/fiftyfirst-experiment/RESULTS.md:47)). Keeping both visible protects against retrospectively selecting a bright orientation; demanding agreement would silently create a harder, unjustified gate.

## Decisions after the complete frozen review

| Complete-study outcome | Supported interpretation | Next action consistent with the frozen design |
|---|---|---|
| New coherent structure meets both-phase/both-family criterion | A reproducible candidate is present under a changed depth window. Ink, correct wrap and anatomical side remain unproven. | Independent review of **all** depth/order views, masks and full-depth CT. Inspect local sheet topology and whether the response could belong to an adjacent wrap before separately designing depth refinement. |
| Structure appears in only one family or phase | An unresolved weak lead; neither a discovery nor evidence that the other family is more accurate. | Archive its exact location and contrary evidence. No expansion or model promotion under this protocol. |
| Responses change strongly but none forms qualifying structure | The perturbation affected the detector; this does not identify why writing remains unrecovered. | Close the three-position screen. Advance PHerc0139 correspondence/benchmark qualification. |
| Outputs remain similar across centres without a qualifying structure | The chosen detector/display remains insensitive to the tested displacement, or shared texture dominates. The tissue may still carry ink. | Same stopping decision. Do not infer adequate surface placement or use confidence to choose a centre. |
| Visually suggestive d0 structure is noticed only during joint review | Retrospective central-depth lead, not the predeclared nonzero-depth success. | Disclose it separately; do not score study52 as passing or silently redefine “not resolved in d0.” |
| Completion/support/identity checks fail | The scientific comparison is not yet evaluable. | Repair or recover the fixed execution with the existing fail-closed rules; no scientific negative conclusion. |

The negative branch should read: **“No candidate met this fixed three-position response criterion in this development strip.”** It should not read “depth was ruled out,” “PHerc0800 is blank,” “the acquisition lacks signal,” or “a new model is required.” The frozen protocol already stops there and moves to PHerc0139 unless new independent physical evidence supplies a specific geometry hypothesis ([protocol](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/in-progress/pherc0800-depth-20261011/source/PROTOCOL.md:113)). That is an appropriate resource-allocation decision without being a mechanistic diagnosis.

## Initial calibration recommendation and its completed follow-up

The following recommendation was subsequently executed under the separate [calibration plan](CALIBRATION_PLAN.md). All eighteen complete boxes and all eighteen matched-size contexts were reviewed; [results](CALIBRATION_RESULTS.md) support broad corroboration for strong structures, with seven uncertain cases. The initial design discussion below is preserved for scope; the actual plan used full-box and context panels, without separate512-cell panel exports. No new inference occurred, and this was not made a prerequisite that delays PHerc0139 qualification.

- Use all eighteen original exposed candidate boxes; no new hand-picked “best letter.” Show each box with a deterministic 1536×512 native-pixel context window centred on its box centre and clamped at image boundaries, plus its three fixed 512-square subcells. The target window is 13.271×4.424 mm at the shared 8.64 µm spacing. Overlapping windows remain overlapping development observations, not eighteen independent samples.
- Use the saved **unmerged** plain and aligned-padded maps, all four models, author reading order and d+1, with the same 0–255 individual-map display and support treatment as study52. Preserve original Hecate mapping to native coordinates. Show the existing published/composed reference only as an explicitly identified comparison panel, never as a pixel-level label.
- Ask whether each reported structure is recognizable at the same source position in both phases and both families under that display. Record all yes/no/uncertain assessments. This tests the corroboration/display portion of the gate, **not** the requirement of improvement over d0, robustness to order, or depth-search sensitivity; those controls are absent from the existing eight-map PHerc343 record ([study46 scope](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/lab/reports/fortysixth-experiment/RESULTS.md:3)).
- Stop after the eighteen boxes. If none or few are recognizable, the current gate has demonstrated poor detectability on its own reported positive reference; retain study52's historical decision but narrow the negative interpretation and redesign a future endpoint before another target search. If the structures are readily corroborated, that establishes limited face validity of this display/gate and still does not estimate sensitivity to unknown PHerc0800 ink.

This is acquisition-setting- and physical-size-matched, **not fully target-matched**: 8.64 µm/116 keV do not guarantee the same reconstruction, contrast, tissue condition, geometry or model ancestry ([source assessment](/workspace/Herculaneum/research/lab/reports/fortyfifth-experiment/STRATEGY_REVIEW.md:20)). No existing independently confirmed PHerc0800 ink region is identified in the reviewed record, so a true target sensitivity calibration is unavailable.

If a later decision genuinely requires a claim that fifteen-voxel centre spacing is adequate, a *different* control would be necessary: freeze one already positive PHerc343 region and measure attenuation/structure retention when its known input centre is displaced in both directions by roughly half that spacing, keeping the same families/phases and all preprocessing fixed. This would be new inference, though much smaller than reproduction. It could demonstrate a blind spot; a successful one-region result would still not guarantee a universal capture radius. **Do not launch it now, and do not make it another automatic CPU gate before paired-acquisition work.**

## Research priority

Finish the unaltered study52, interpret it narrowly, and qualify the PHerc0139 paired acquisitions in parallel. The highest-value unresolved issue is the relation between observations, labels and acquisition transfer; additional target confidence maps cannot identify that relationship by themselves. The inexpensive saved-map calibration above closes a concrete interpretive gap without reopening the rejected adapters, fitting a new display, claiming a holdout, or consuming a GPU budget.
