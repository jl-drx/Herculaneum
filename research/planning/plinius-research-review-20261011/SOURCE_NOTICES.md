# Source attribution and scope

The PHerc0139 CT observations, volume metadata and derived slice panels come
from the Vesuvius Challenge open-data bucket. The retained volume catalogue
specifies [CC BY-NC4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Request
ledgers retain exact origin URLs, timestamps and SHA256s. Keep that attribution
and licence when reusing the data or derivative figures.

The PHerc343 maps are this project's retained reproductions of the pinned
[Nieuwlaar PHerc343 recipe](https://github.com/Nieuwlaar/pherc343-first-letters/tree/e8370f1f88a211166056df62ea6fcfffe4a1063e),
using previously recorded public source/model/data terms. Its published composite
appears only as a labelled comparison in the review panels; it is not ground
truth. No new author map or CT was fetched for this calibration.

Pinned source excerpts from
[Nieuwlaar's native-dense repository](https://github.com/Nieuwlaar/ink9um-dense-native/tree/217e7521670db49cde97872ea6a8bdfb80c449f1)
