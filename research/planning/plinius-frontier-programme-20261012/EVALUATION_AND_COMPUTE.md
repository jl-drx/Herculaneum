## Three different reserves

1. **Development:** all previously inspected Paris/Frag6/PHerc343/PHerc0800
   regions, all repeated public validation benchmarks, and the current 0139
   cohort after its locked review. Public surface challenge cases can qualify
   methods and metrics, with their training exposure declared.
2. **Geometry confirmation:** physically separated CT blocks with independent
   sheet-instance/trace annotations and withheld checking anchors. Reserve
   contiguous paths as well as isolated cubes; do not let disjoint file IDs
   disguise neighbouring/overlapping tissue. Include easy and difficult strata,
   tears and negative examples of plausible but incorrect joins.
3. **Ink/text confirmation:** new physical support and preferably new parents
   with independently supported annotations, and separate unopened target
   surfaces for source-supported qualitative discovery. A previously unviewed
   crop can still have been in a released model's training lineage. Report
   local exposure, gradient exposure, teacher ancestry and annotation independence
   separately. Unknown upstream exposure cannot be called proved external holdout.

**Reservation is an immediate next deliverable, not an accomplished claim in this
review.** We have not silently declared new coordinates fresh. The pre-existing
0139 reserved contexts remain protected. Create a metadata/geometry-only reserve
manifest with scroll/fragment parent, scan, world-coordinate support, all aliases,
mesh version, source bounding envelope, teacher context and model receptive field.
Expand for interpolation, augmentation, normal sweeps, tile context and measured
registration uncertainty. Check actual physical intersections, not just crop
rectangles or centre distance. Reserve before labels/scores are opened; only
development material drives method and threshold choice.

If independent labels cannot yet be obtained, experiments may proceed with
explicit teacher/annotation-agreement claims. That limits the claim rather than
forcing an endless wait. It does not permit calling the resulting score ink
accuracy or reusing those regions later as untouched confirmation.

## Supervision hierarchy

| Evidence | Defensible use | Required qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Exposed-fragment IR/optical ink | Strong 2D ink support | Independently checked CT/optical registration, support masks and parent lineage |
| Directly visible CT deposits with expert review | Volumetric ink development/reference, notably some Paris4 regions | Physical deposit/ink interpretation and uncertainty; do not generalize its scan regime to all scrolls |
| CT-reviewed sheet/fibre instances | Geometry tracing/placement reference | Same-sheet identity, face/medial distinction, ambiguous regions, withheld checking anchors |
| Specialist-reviewed sealed-scroll strokes | Ink/text evaluation with provenance | What imagery/models informed the annotation; adjudication and uncertainty |
| Public native binary labels | Native annotation agreement | Declared supervision masks; original annotation/teacher history may be incomplete |
| Fine or coarse model predictions | Pseudo-supervision and teacher agreement | Same physical tissue, transformation uncertainty, teacher ancestry, false negatives and unknown support |
| Unlabelled, dark or low-response regions | Unsupervised data or unknown | Never automatically a confirmed negative/blank |

Follow the source-specific target contract rather than silently merging masks.
Before adopting a new production loader, counterfactually alter excluded labels
and require unchanged training inputs, membership, targets, loss and gradients.
The published native-supervision isolation fix is relevant prior art [S19]; this
review does not assert that the frozen bespoke 0139 runner or any released
checkpoint was affected. Do not patch a running experiment on that assumption.

## Measure an improvement we actually want

- **Geometry:** unique correct-sheet area in physical units, connected path
  length, wrong-sheet switches, merges/splits, self-intersections, missing areas,
  uncertainty/abstention, placement/normal error, flattening distortion and
  correction minutes. Compare coverage at matched error and prompt/time budgets.
  Account for duplicate patches and rejected hard cases in the original denominator.
- **Ink:** equal-parent AP, per-parent AP/AUC, threshold-fixed precision/recall,
  calibration, supervised-negative response, and false-positive rates on
  independently supported negative material. Preserve single-class regions
  without fabricating their AP/AUC. Teacher agreement is a separate table.
- **Robustness:** physically registered perturbation curves, wrong-sheet
  contamination and fixed grid-origin behaviour. A correlation or top-response
  overlap is a robustness metric, not ink accuracy.
- **Text:** fixed-display, blinded paired review of all preselected contexts,
  source-linked strokes and specialist-supported letters/lines, including
  uncertainty and rejected readings. Keep a separate practical surface-area/
  reading-effort endpoint. Do not train the recovery model on OCR/lexical guesses
  or select a mesh because it forms a plausible word.

Report physical region and parent results, not pixel-level pseudo-replication.
Use paired training seeds for treatment effects. Block resampling can describe
spatial uncertainty conditional on a scroll; it cannot manufacture between-scroll
evidence. Parent-held-out assessment and training-seed uncertainty answer
different questions. Released checkpoint differences are not pure seed noise.

The draft programme's +0.02 AP, retention, coverage and time margins are pilot
utility thresholds. Before confirmation, use development variability and label
uncertainty to choose cohort size and a fixed practical effect. Do not call one
arm pair powered because it meets an arbitrary threshold or has millions of
pixels. Report uncertainty intervals and all strata; reserve final claims for
replication. Do not change an old failed gate retrospectively.

## Parallel work and escalation

| Work | Suitable now alongside the CPU worker | Later escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Literature/code/data-manifest audit, source lineage, reserve construction | Yes; light CPU/network, no model prediction | None unless new annotation/access is needed |
| Saved geometry checks, tiny fixtures, independent metric implementation | Yes, with bounded threads and memory | Bigger CPU meshing after the worker, or spare VM only when hour-scale parallel work is useful |
| New 3D surface/ink baseline inference | Prepare exact inputs/config now; avoid competing heavy CPU execution | Approval for bounded GPU batch after price/runtime/memory qualification |
| Coverage or label-quality training pair | Prepare matched schedule and sources now | GPU pilot; later multi-seed replication under a separately agreed budget |
| Global reconstruction / large self-supervised pretraining | Not an immediate task | Only after local evidence and a reviewed compute/data proposal |

All runtime ranges in PROGRAMME.md are order-of-magnitude planning estimates,
not quotes or benchmarks. A5000/3090-class 24 GB cards are plausible for many
ink/inference jobs; m7 training, large volumetric heads or full global fitting
may need more memory. Use measured maximum memory and effective throughput,
including data loading and checkpoints, to choose a device. H100 is not the
default. Native worker CPU use and its locked outputs remain unaffected.

**No new paid compute without explicit approval.** The existing £10 total first
GPU pilot cap covers every attempt, retry and attributable storage/transfer/tax/
currency margin. It is a ceiling, not advance permission under this new request.
Before asking, make the workload concrete: pinned CUDA/container and model/data
hashes, bounded inputs, scientific endpoint, conservative current cost estimate,
validated result/checkpoint delivery, and an independent shutdown mechanism.
Any paid timing pilot is itself paid compute requiring approval. Synthetic/CPU
timing cannot certify GPU throughput; disclose that uncertainty and cap the
initial measured segment. No implicit paid restarts.
