# Plinius: a broader research programme

12 October 2026. Research recommendation, not an experiment launch or a frozen
protocol. The native PHerc0139 nine-fit study and its locked evaluation are
unchanged. It is one baseline for learning from native-frame annotations, not a
gate that every future method must pass. No paid compute is authorized here.

**Recommendation: pursue correct-sheet recovery and broad native ink learning
as parallel priorities, supported by targeted annotation and fresh evaluation.**
The most credible contribution may be better training data, sheet connectivity,
or an economical correction workflow. A new architecture is only one option.
We cannot yet identify geometry, acquisition, or learning as the dominant cause
of PHerc0800's weak outputs.

## What changes in our strategy

Our engineering has established source correspondence, reproducible rendering,
model execution, restartable training, and several useful negative controls.
It has not established an independent ink benchmark, broad acquisition transfer,
or an improvement in recovered physical surface. The programme became too
dependent on small training supports and repeatedly inspected regions. A long
sequence of input qualifications also displaced experiments that could directly
measure improvement. More qualifications are not, by themselves, progress toward
reading a scroll.

The 8 October review already identified surface reconstruction as a separate
track. We did not develop it into a substantial learning or geometry comparison;
the intervening work chiefly assessed rendering, depth, and correspondence. This
agenda makes that separate track concrete, rather than claiming it was never
considered. Historical negative decisions remain valid within their scope.

The latest releases strengthen the case for changing priorities:

- **Reader v2** adds broad native coarse supervision with the existing hybrid
  architecture. It is a necessary contemporary ink comparator alongside
  dense-native and Hecate. Its results are author-reported, have one training
  seed, mix human-label and teacher-map endpoints, and use evaluation scores for
  development decisions. Its prediction/teacher alignment check is not a
  CT-based sheet-registration certificate.
- **Socratic F0** uses an ordinary m7 architecture with a broader corpus and
  distillation. It reports better coarse surface scores, but its main benchmark
  uses fine-teacher targets and selects checkpoints/operating points. The
  human-label comparison does not show the same advantage. Surface overlap is
  also not the same as correct connected mesh area.
- **Stevens' patch assembly, ScrollFiesta, Lasagna, spiral fitting, winding
  constraint audits, PLatS, and neural/fiber tracers** cover much of the obvious
  geometry design space already. We should compare against them and target a
  measured residual failure, not rename their mechanisms.
- **Hecate already combines volumetric ink, learned depth attention, and
  fine-to-coarse/native supervision.** Generic depth attention or distillation
  is not a new research contribution.

These are source and code findings, not new reproductions of those systems.
The [source audit](SOURCES.md) separates available artifacts from methods whose
current documentation still points to private checkpoints or incomplete data.

## Ranked priorities

| Rank | Direction | Why it deserves attention | Immediate discriminating work |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Correct-sheet recovery, topology and connected coverage | An excellent ink detector cannot read material omitted from a mesh or joined to another wrap. We have barely tested this stage. | Compare existing surface fields and tracers on fixed, CT-reviewed difficult blocks; measure identity errors and useful area, then test one targeted repair. |
| 2 | Broad, acquisition-matched native ink learning | Strong recent models improve through supervision and coverage without new architectures. Our earlier continuation studies did not test this at an informative scale. | Audit/reproduce Reader v2's input and evaluation contract; prepare a coverage comparison with original-source exposure fixed. |
| 3 | Annotation quality and targeted correction | Surface meshes and sealed-scroll ink labels are approximate; pseudo-labels can reproduce the teacher's mistakes. | Measure disagreement on development support; compare targeted correction with equal-time random correction. |
| 4 | Geometry-conditioned, sheet-specific ink prediction | Neighbouring-wrap contamination has a credible mechanism, but generic depth adapters and averaging are already covered. | Test independently specified sheet identity using Hecate's 3D output, with wrong-sheet and matched-smoothing controls. |
| 5 | Acquisition/reconstruction robustness | Nominal voxel size conceals energy, phase, reconstruction and intensity differences. | Use existing same-scan reconstruction variants for a bounded robustness test; reserve paired-acquisition training for admitted correspondence. |
| 6 | Better representations and compute-efficient training | Released pretrained 3D features and well-trained U-Nets are stronger hypotheses than another tiny residual head. | GPU-appropriate frozen-feature probe, then matched fine-tuning if warranted; depth/jitter efficiency is a separate factorial test. |
| 7 | Local placement, normals and sampling | Can improve inputs cheaply, but cannot recover missing sheets or repair connectivity. | A CT-anchored placement/normal sensitivity test with renderer and tile origin separated. |

The order reflects expected research value and our current evidence, not a
claim that rank 1 has already been proved to be PHerc0800's main bottleneck.
Ranks 1–3 should start together. Rank 7 is a bounded supporting task, not another
programme-length sequence of depth sweeps.

## What to do alongside the current run

1. Build a **geometry development packet** from previously exposed material and
   public instance/surface data. Fix easy, curved, touching-sheet and damaged
   strata before comparing outputs. First compare released methods; do not
   start by training a new surface network.
2. Prepare a **modern native-ink baseline packet**: Reader v2, dense-native,
   Hecate, and a fixed released checkpoint/ensemble control. Check training
   lineage, actual physical sampling and supervision exclusions. Choose any
   model or ensemble on separate development material.
3. Create a **physical exposure and annotation registry** for the broader
   programme, extending existing ledgers. Reserve confirmation material by
   geometry/metadata before viewing its labels or model scores. Existing 0139
   evaluation remains sealed; after its scheduled review it is development
   evidence for subsequent studies, never a renewed untouched test.

These are independent of the current result. Source inspection, metadata,
existing-map analysis and small CPU geometry checks can proceed locally without
another VM. Heavy builds or inference should wait for idle CPU capacity or a
separately approved GPU pilot. No spare-VM assignment has been made.

The first paid proposal should be a **bounded inference and geometry comparison**
if new surface/model predictions are necessary, or a **matched native training
arm pair** if the baseline/data packet is already sufficient. Measure throughput
and choose the more informative workload; do not spend the allowance just to
validate a container. The £10 total pilot cap remains, but it is not a claim
that £10 can fund a statistically convincing research programme.

## Scope and preservation

This packet changes research priorities, not the running study. It contains no
new CT payloads, weights, predictions, training, GPU job, public release, or
Actions dispatch. The archived source review is private and checksum-bound.
[REVIEW_RECEIPT.json](REVIEW_RECEIPT.json) records the reviewed local evidence,
source snapshot and worker checks. [SOURCE_REQUESTS.json](SOURCE_REQUESTS.json)
retains failed guessed URLs as well as the successful canonical replacements.

The proposed numeric gates are practical pilot decisions, not retrospective
changes to previous gates or claims of statistical power. Each experiment needs
its own final cohort, code, baseline and endpoint freeze before execution.
