6. CRITIQUE OF THE ADDITIONAL SIX-MAP RANK/OVERLAP STUDY
Useful question: are spatial rankings or high-response locations stable across the saved model/phase maps? This complements raw-level differences and can reveal calibration effects. It supplies no ground truth.
- Freeze valid support, all pairs, top-fractions/threshold rules and tied-rank handling before execution. uint8 and clipped values create many ties. Exact top-k membership can depend on arbitrary coordinate tie-breaking; threshold-inclusive sets have unequal sizes. Report the actual sizes and tie mass, or tie-robust bounds, rather than conceal this.
- Rank correlation discards calibration and saturation; high overlap can coexist with large amplitude or phase differences. Rank agreement is not accuracy and does not identify the better model.
- Pixel autocorrelation, repeated phases of the same physical CT and related models invalidate independent-pixel significance claims. Avoid nominal millions-of-pixels p-values and treating six maps as six independent biological replicates.
- Compare matched physical coordinates; separate within-model phase agreement from cross-model agreement. Same valid support matters. Top-fraction overlap has a density-dependent baseline; report the definition and a baseline as descriptive only, not an independence test.
- All18 boxes must remain included; do not promote the highest-overlap boxes or infer letter identity. These are previously exposed references, not an untouched test set.
- PNG-derived yellow masks encode the original four-model styled ensemble and annotations. They are not independent labels. Selecting six-map metrics because they best match that PNG introduces circularity.
- Freeze the six-map study before Hecate and report it separately. Do not tune weights, fractions or endpoint using it, then present the Hecate result as confirmatory.
