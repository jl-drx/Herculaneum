# Prospective physical-family reserve

The frozen `REGISTRY.json` protects PHerc0175A and PHerc0175B together as one
conservative numeric family, and PHerc1203 as the other. All source volumes,
future meshes, fine/native aliases and teacher contexts from those families
remain out of new development/training. Only catalogue records and array headers
have been opened; there is no new CT, surface mask, ink map or label from them.

Selection is metadata-based. PHerc1203 is the previously identified native/fine
lead. PHerc0175 A/B is the lowest-numbered 116-keV family outside Reader's declared
training families, F0's train/validation roles and its published eight-scroll
geometry audit. This rule was checked against the complete fetched catalogue
and registry. A/B grouping is conservative and does not assert exact physical
adjacency. Whole-family exclusion does not require a guessed fine/native affine.

Reader v2's released config contains neither family. The F0 registry calls all
three samples unseen. These are bounded lineage statements, not proof of absence
from m7, another ink model, a teacher, a private training corpus or public human
inspection. Upstream ancestry remains unknown. PHerc1203's documented fine/native
pair is not assumed registered: the current catalogue supplies no transform.

The local metadata/name audit considered 17,392 JSON/JSONL files, finding no
identified prior acquired payload receipt or named scientific artifact for the
reserves. Four large historical metadata files were then scanned separately:
121,811,403 bytes, with no reserve-name mentions. The original audit's
`REVIEW_REQUIRED` records that size limit; `LARGE_METADATA_FOLLOWUP.json` resolves
those four omissions. Neither search proves absence from anonymous caches,
unrecorded devices or upstream models. No array bodies were opened for the audit;
the running native-learning/source directories were excluded.

This is **a prospective embargo, not a ready evaluation benchmark**. Independent
reference annotations, actual context/support qualification and a separate
confirmation freeze are still required. Evaluation must distinguish physical
parent variation from seed variation and use only development evidence for
method/threshold selection. If independent labels are unavailable, retain a
limited teacher/annotation-agreement claim; do not manufacture ink truth.

`registry.py` is a family-scope guard for new intake manifests. Nine checks cover
reserved A/B/fragment aliases, the protected native0139 family, metadata-only
access, legacy Scroll1 identity and refusal of unresolved/unassigned families.
It does not globally intercept tools, replace geometric footprint checks for
within-parent splits, or authorize a scientific training/evaluation cohort.
