## Portable map review

`python verify_saved_maps.py --packet /path/to/completion-review` verifies the
saved input hashes, independently recomputes the full set of metrics, and
checks the fixed-display PNG pixels. It is read-only and prints its result to
stdout. It resolves historical pins through the included mapping manifest,
so it needs neither the original `/workspace` paths nor the full checkpoint.
Python with NumPy and Pillow is sufficient; no model weights or GPU are used.

The historical `assess_complete.py` and its absolute pins are unchanged.
The portable checker interprets those pins through the saved mapping rather
than silently rewriting them.
