# Principal review: seed42 and four-model overviews

11 October 2026. Reviewed only after all32 shifted maps and16 baseline views
were restored and independently verified. No inference or contrast adjustment.
This judgment was written before reading the other reviewers' conclusions.

Inspected the four contact sheets (dnative,seed42,seed43,Hecate), all four
seed42 full-strip panels (plain/padded × forward/reverse), and all12 seed42
fixed-cell panels (same combinations × cells1–3). Every panel contains d−15,
d0,d+15 in that order, fixed0–255. Full-strip tool display was uniformly reduced
from1568×1702 to1504×1632 by the viewer; fixed-cell panels were also opened
separately. No unlisted image or fitted enhancement was used.

**Seed42 does not supply a convincing newly resolved coherent stroke group
meeting the frozen criterion.** Both depth movement and padding clearly change
responses. In cell1 at d+15, stronger patches recur around the lower-left and
lower-middle portions. Forward plain renders these as separated diffuse lobes;
forward padded changes their strength/connectivity substantially. Reverse views
show a broader bright cluster through this area. Their positions are informative
response correspondence, but I cannot identify a stable fine stroke organization
or recurring text-like grouping across the required views. This is not a claim
that the structures are known non-ink.

Cell2 has an arc/open-loop-like diffuse feature in forward views at d−15/d0 and
several displaced/changed bright lobes at d+15. These do not form a new coherent
group with stable connectivity. Cell3 has isolated vertical/oval responses,
including stronger lower/right-edge features at d+15; the feature organization
varies with phase/order and does not provide the required new group. The tiny
brown upper-right support exclusion is visible and was not scored as absence.

Hecate's overview shows a strong d+15 response in the left region, and seed43's
overview also strengthens there. Bright co-location is not sufficient by itself
to call that a coherent stroke group. Detailed Hecate/dnative and seed43 reviews
are assigned separately; final synthesis must incorporate them rather than use
seed42 alone as a required model-specific veto. The protocol needs both model
families, not all individual checkpoints or reversal invariance.

My recommendation from these inspected views is no candidate promotion from
seed42. Preserve the contrary d+15 left-region response and all48 maps. No depth
or region expansion follows from this judgment. It does not rule out ink, other
depths, other tissue, or a weak signal below the current recipe's recovery ability.
