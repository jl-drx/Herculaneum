## Completed native baseline

The native PHerc0139 nine-fit CPU study [completed unchanged on 12 October](in-progress/pherc0139-native-learning-20261011/completion-20261012/README.md).
The separate verifier reproduced all fifteen maps' metrics and frozen decisions.
Training fit is strong, but all three winding transfer gates fail; the mean AP
gain over the profile baseline is −0.013140. All fits hit the update ceiling,
so a no-signal conclusion is unsupported. Do not extend these fits or choose a
different endpoint after viewing their results. This is now exposed development
evidence, not a fresh confirmation set or a veto on adequately designed GPU
training. Strong native baselines and controlled broader coverage remain next.

## Independent work now

1. **Correct-sheet recovery:** construct a bounded development intake for surface
   fields, instances and tracing. Separate detector comparisons from tracer
   comparisons. Measure physical coverage and identity mistakes, including tears,
   rather than selecting geometry for a larger ink response.
2. **Strong native learning:** use the completed common-support comparison to
   retain dense-native and Hecate as complementary strong controls. Reader v2
   remains an executable training recipe and comparator, not the automatic best
   baseline. Prepare a paired coverage study with original
   source draws and source loss weights held fixed. The [synthetic preflight](in-progress/native-training-preflight-20261012/README.md)
   passes exclusion/augmentation checks and shows that a mixed-batch BCE denominator
   can dilute old-source gradients despite identical draws. Use separate stream
   normalization with fixed coefficients in both arms. Use adequate exposure and
   an explicit GPU proposal.
3. **Reference and exposure:** extend the physical registry, distinguish local
   exposure from upstream training/teacher exposure, and reserve new support
   using geometry/metadata before inspecting labels or predictions. Build targeted
   correction as a separately controlled data intervention.
